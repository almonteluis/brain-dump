# Levels of Energy Scale

## Core Premise

There are clearly definable, perceivable and achievable "levels" of energy. Each level corresponds with specific physical, mental, emotional, and spiritual realities.

**The Scale (1-1000):**
- Energy levels as gradients of one continuum
- Like temperature hot/cold - different gradients of same thing
- Context-dependent meaning (not dualistic good/bad)

## Energy Level Attributes

Each level manifests in:
- Specific states of mind and emotion
- Specific words, behaviors, and actions
- Specific surroundings, environments, objects, and places
- Specific qualities, moods, and intentions
- Specific methods of "moving upward"
- Specific modes of perception and truth

These are also levels of consciousness and emotion, or "vibration" - energy states that give off a certain radiation.

# Key Principles

## Spectral Perception vs Dualistic Perception

**Dualism:** "X is good, Y is bad" - only two possibilities
**Spectral Perception:** Allows for gradients, context-dependent meanings
- Hot/cold analogy: Different temperatures of same continuum
- "Good" vs "Bad" becomes dependent on context and energy level
- Example: Level 700 (Bliss) for politician vs Level 400 (Intellect) for parent

## Absolutism vs Relativism

| Perspective | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Absolutist (correct) | There is truth closer to source and further away from truth |
| Relativist (incorrect) | "Nothing is good or bad. It's only your opinion" |
| Both (flawed) | Seeing two things without spectral discernment |

## "Higher" vs "Lower" Terminology

Avoid judgmental language:
- Use "appropriate/inappropriate" instead of "good/bad"
- Example: Level 700 (Bliss) may be inappropriate for parenting; Level 400 (Intellect) may be inappropriate for child

**Important:** Appreciate that everything has its rightful place in Infinity

# Effort and Effortlessness

**Core Concept:** Positive states don't require hard work, effort, or long climb.

## The Cork Analogy

- Pushing cork underwater = Effort (negative state requires effort)
- Letting cork float = Effortlessness (positive state, allowing, natural)
- **Principle:** Allow, release, relax vs. Push, force, struggle

## Paradox of Doing Less

- 80% of self-help products involve relaxing/repackaged as "special methods"
- Many people pay thousands for tools that essentially teach: relax your mind
- **Wisdom:** To be willing to relax fully and work fully are both essential
- Don't "work hard at relaxing" - Mastered by many

## Be-Do-Have (vs. Do-Have)

| Aspect | Low Levels (200) | Mid Levels (200-450) |
|---------|---------------------|----------------------|
| Focus | Having | Being |
| Motivation | "To get" (fear, lack) | Doing, creating |

# Scale of Consciousness Evolution

## Natural Sequences

The scale reflects natural sequences of human emotion, spiritual growth, or healing processes.

**Evolutionary Flow:**
- Lower levels: Struggle, effort, resistance, need, survival
- Mid levels: Action, doing, achievement, growth
- High levels: Being, presence, awareness, unconditional love
- Highest levels: Transcendence, divine consciousness

## Backsliding

- Moving UP scale is easy (natural flow)
- Moving DOWN scale can happen quickly (anger from 190 to 160)
- Understanding these patterns increases compassion for others' suffering

## Exceptions and Variations

- Some may skip certain levels temporarily
- Trauma can cause temporary state shifts
- "Heaven or hell" - Rapid transitions possible from higher perspective (600+)
- Energy levels affect relationship to same reality (e.g., long beach vs airplane)

# Practical Exercises

## 10-Second Energy Release

1. Imagine investing lot of effort → relax with that thought
2. Imagine relaxing, non-doing, effortlessness → relax with thought
3. Imagine effort → release aversion/resistance, enjoy it
4. Imagine effortlessness, laziness, vacation-ism → release aversion
5. Again imagine effort → actually enjoy it
6. And again imagine effortlessness
7. Be OK with both (effort + effortlessness)
8. Repeat 10 times before reading on

**Purpose:** Release aversion toward both effort and effortlessness

- "When I find a partner/make money/get weekend/go to God" = conditional well-being
- Conditional happiness = puppet of world, controlled by external circumstances
- ATM example: Emotional state changes when money withdrawn

3. Notice how "wanting approval" creates a lack
4. Release focus from the entanglement

**Insight:** Independence of all conditions (except enlightenment/divinity) is prerequisite for true well-being

**Instructions:**
1. Deep breath (longer than in-breath)
2. Out-breath through nose, open mouth
3. Sound "Aaaaaaaaah"
4. Welcome everything perceived/felt/seen/heard

# Energy Dynamics in Relationships

## Teaching Different Energy Levels

Author's experience conducting workshops with groups at different energy levels.

### Level 70-110 (Welfare Group)
- **State:** Apathy, depression, hopelessness, self-pity, fear
- **Physical manifestations:** Taking drugs, exhausted eyes, slow speech, difficulty focusing
- **Environment:** Shabby furniture, broken coffee machine, paint crumbling
- **Energy mismatch:** Seminar room featured in abundance, but attendees radiated scarcity
- **Teaching method:** Bottled-up emotions + patient listening + physical touch (hugs)
- **Result:** Participants carried up to Level 180 (antagonism, pride, complaint)
- **Outcome:** Success with relief, one person's comment: "I feel much better"

### Level 300 (Business Group)
- **State:** Dreams, ambition, orderliness, conservatism
- **Corporate setting:** Weekend course in offices
- **Teaching approach:** Level 500 topics, no emotional confrontation
- **Results:** Successful, participants enjoyed "feeling alive" and "feeling connected to energy"

### Level 450 (Performers - Actors/Dancers)
- **Energy Level:** Would become bored without stimulation
- **Method:** Long etheric (very relaxed state)
- **Activities:** Meditation, Unconditional Love games, identity shifting
- **Outcome:** "I feel so much more alive after this workshop"

## Key Insights

- Shift state appropriately for audience
- Level 500 teachings to Level 400 group = Inappropriate
- "Life goals" + "action steps" = Wrong approach for Level 300 group
- Different demeanor, vocabulary, clothing for different energy levels
