Workout cheat codes I know at 45 that I wish I knew at 25:

1. Take 5 grams of creatine for the muscles. Take 10-20 grams of creatine for the brain. ^931282187

[View Highlight](https://twitter.com/FitFounder/status/1960364468971483516)

---

2. For a great workout, mix a bottle of beetroot juice with half a teaspoon of baking soda. Beetroot boosts nitric oxide for endurance; baking soda buffers acid, delaying fatigue for workouts.

3. When working out use swear words to enhance your ability to lift heavier. ^931282188

[View Highlight](https://twitter.com/FitFounder/status/1960364471647432771)

---

4. Between your sets, go for a walk to improve recovery and increase step count.

5. If you're stuck for time, you can cut your workout time in half while still getting the same gains by incorporating supersets for non-competing exercises. ^931282189

[View Highlight](https://twitter.com/FitFounder/status/1960364474352779458)

6. Another way to cut time in the gym is to use Myo-reps. Do one hard set near failure (12-15 reps), rest 5 breaths, then do 3–5 mini-sets of 3–5 reps each. Use only for accessory movements.

7. When lifting, count backwards from your rep target to complete the desired number of reps. ^931282190

[View Highlight](https://twitter.com/FitFounder/status/1960364477091615224)

8. For hypertrophy, inhale on eccentric and exhale on concentric phase, using deep belly breaths. For strength, use belly breaths, hold & brace core, (Valsalva) during heavy lifts.

9. Drink water with a pinch of sea salt or electrolyte powder to stay properly hydrated. ^931282191

[View Highlight](https://twitter.com/FitFounder/status/1960364479947923791)

10. For pushing exercises, emphasize force from your thumb side to engage pressing muscles.

11. For pulling, focus on your pinky side to better activate your back. ^931282192

12. Exercise right for your body type. Choose exercises that suit limb length and torso ratios; long legs may favor deadlifts, short arms help pressing, and adjust technique or variations for safest, most effective results.

18. Improve recovery with post-workout box breathing:

Step 1: Breathe in, counting to 4 slowly. Feel the air enter your lungs.
Step 2: Hold your breath for 4 seconds.
Step 3: Slowly exhale through your mouth for 4 seconds.
Step 4: Repeat steps 1 to 3 until you feel centered. ^931282196
