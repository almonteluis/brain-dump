# Purple (Violet) Energy

## Physical Properties

- **Shortest wavelength** in visible spectrum
- **Highest frequency** in visible spectrum
- **Limit of visible energy** — beyond is ultraviolet (invisible)

## Historical Significance

- Purple dye was rare and expensive → became color of royalty
- Represents wisdom, spirituality, and divine connection
- Associated with the crown chakra — unity with the divine

## Spiritual Context

### Crown Chakra
- **Location**: Top of head
- **Represents**: Enlightenment, unity with divine, intuition
- **Connection**: Violet is the bridge to the unseen/spiritual realm

### Hidden Meaning
Purple represents the limit of what we can see — but there's more beyond. It's the last color we can perceive, yet energy continues beyond.

## Key Insight

> "Purple literally represents the limit of visible energy... there's more out there that we can't even see, but it's still there."

## Practical Uses

**Use purple when:**
- Meditation or spiritual work
- Dream journaling
- Intuition development
- Connecting with higher purpose
