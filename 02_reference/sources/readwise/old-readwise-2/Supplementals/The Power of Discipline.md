When you make healthy choices, self-discipline is strengthened; on the other hand, making unhealthy choices diminishes self-discipline. ^928810618

---

The trick is to keep reminding yourself that the more you get up early, the easier it will become. ^928810619

---

The core of self-discipline is doing what you need to do whether you feel like it or not. ^928810620

---

A self-disciplined person is confident because, regardless of where they are at that moment, they know that they are the best version of themselves. They are eating properly, exercising, and working towards their goals. They feel good about life because they are in the driver’s seat, and they know exactly where they are going. ^928810621

---

Therefore, the best way to build self-discipline is to remove yourself from temptation. ^928810622

---

If you can start seeing urges and temptations with curiosity rather than fear, changing your behavior will become a lot easier. Spend time studying your own habits and pay attention to your urges. Once you master urge surfing, you can overcome any addiction. ^928810623

---

But the 40% rule states that when the mind starts telling the body it’s tired, we have only reached 40% of what we are capable of. At this point, it’s up to you to choose to believe that you’ve still got another 60% left in you. ^928810624

---

Discipline is about doing what you know you’ve got to do even when you don’t feel like it. But for your efforts to make a difference, you must be consistent. Slacking off every other day isn’t going to get you to your destination any more quickly. Consistency builds momentum and that’s how dreams become a reality. When you are aware of what discipline demands, you are more likely to choose to do the right thing. ^928810625
