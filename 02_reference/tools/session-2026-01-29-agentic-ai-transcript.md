## Key Frameworks Extracted

### The Governance Formula
```
Orchestration + Observability + Auditability = Governance
```

### Three Types of Gaps
1. **Coordination Gap** — Everyone gives different answers for AI success
2. **Orchestration Gap** — Metrics don't connect to business outcomes
3. **Governance/Auditability Gap** — Can't explain AI decisions' effects on other systems

### Decision Velocity Equation
```
Decision Velocity = (Automation + Coordination) × Confidence
```

## Core Insights

### 1. The Competitive Shift (2026)
Winners won't be determined by who has the most AI — they'll be defined by who figured out orchestration, observability, and auditability.

### 2. Governance is Speed
Companies treating governance as features (not constraints) move **5-7x faster** than competitors stuck in manual approval chains.

### 3. Orchestration ≠ Integration
Real orchestration means AI systems understand context across domains, not just API plumbing.

### 4. The Foundational Question
"What data can we trust, and what do we need to fix before we automate decisions at scale?"

## Action Items

- [ ] Assess organization: Which gap do we have? (coordination/orchestration/governance)
- [ ] Audit AI systems: Do we have observability built in?
- [ ] Map decision loops: Where could decision velocity compound?
- [ ] Evaluate coordination: Do AI systems work together or in isolation?
