## Validation Request

Under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), 15 U.S.C. § 1692g(b), I have the right to request validation of this debt. I am requesting that you provide the following information:

1. **Verification of Debt**
   - The amount of the debt
   - The name of the creditor
   - Proof that you are authorized to collect this debt

2. **Documentation**
   - A copy of the original agreement or contract
   - Account statements from the original creditor
   - Any assignment or transfer documents showing you own this debt

3. **License Information**
   - Your debt collection license number
   - State of licensure

## Response Deadline

Please provide this information within **30 days** of receiving this letter. Per FDCPA, you must cease collection activities until you provide proper validation.
