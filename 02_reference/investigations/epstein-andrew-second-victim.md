# Second Epstein victim claims she was sent to UK for sex with Andrew, lawyer says

## Summary

A second woman alleges she was sent to the UK by Jeffrey Epstein for a sexual encounter with Prince Andrew (Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor) at his Royal Lodge residence in 2010.

---

## Key Facts

### The Allegation
- **Year:** 2010
- **Location:** Royal Lodge, Prince Andrew's residence on Windsor estate
- **Victim:** Non-British woman, in her 20s at time
- **Sequence:** Sexual encounter at Royal Lodge, then tour of Buckingham Palace and tea
- **Lawyer:** Brad Edwards, US firm Edwards Henderson
- **Edwards represents:** 200+ Epstein survivors worldwide

### Previous Legal Contact
- Communications existed between victim and Andrew before encounter
- Edwards contacted Andrew's US legal counsel
- Andrew "seemingly disconnected from his lawyers"
- Communications stopped after King Charles stripped Andrew's royal titles (October 2025)

---

## Virginia Giuffre Connection

### Giuffre's Allegations
- **2001:** Brought to London to have sex with Andrew when she was 17
- **2001-2002:** Forced to have sex with Andrew 3 times total:
  1. London (2001)
  2. New York
  3. Epstein's private Caribbean island (Little St. James)

### Legal Outcome
- **2021:** Giuffre filed civil lawsuit against Andrew in US
- **February 2022:** Case settled for estimated £12m
- **2025:** Giuffre took her own life

### Royal Family Response
- **October 2025:** King Charles stripped Andrew of "style, title and honours"
- **Reason:** Following publication of Giuffre's memoir
- **Palace statement:** Andrew left Royal Lodge (his home since 2004)
- **Palace quote:** "These censures are deemed necessary, notwithstanding the fact that he continues to deny allegations"

### Lawyer's Criticism
Brad Edwards criticized royal family's response:
> "The idea that the Royal Family so far cares about the victims, wanted to make things right - what they have done by simply stripping Prince Andrew of his titles and nothing more, has had the exact opposite effect of what they claim they are trying to do."

**Impact:** Stripping titles allowed Andrew to claim "no money and no ability to provide compensation" - "allow these women to just suffer"
