# Drowning death of crypto 'visionary' Nikolai Mushegian fuels conspiracy theories

## Summary

Nikolai Mushegian, a 29-year-old cryptocurrency pioneer and early developer of Maker DAO, tweeted that intelligence agencies were going to murder him — and was found dead on a Puerto Rico beach hours later.

---

## Key Facts

### The Death
- **Date:** October 28, 2022
- **Location:** Ashford Beach, San Juan, Puerto Rico (near his $6 million Condado beach house)
- **Time:** Found a little after 9 a.m.
- **Circumstances:** Body discovered by a surfer in waves; wearing clothes, had wallet
- **Beach Warning:** Local hotels warn against ocean swimming due to riptides
- **Police Status:** Still under investigation, not considered a homicide at present

### The Final Tweet (4:57 a.m., same day)

> "CIA and Mossad and pedo elite are running some kind of sex trafficking entrapment blackmail ring out of Puerto Rico and Caribbean islands. They are going to frame me with a laptop planted by my ex gf who was a spy. They will torture me to death."

---

## Background

### Who Was Nikolai Mushegian?
- **Age:** 29
- **Background:** 2014 Carnegie Mellon graduate
- **Role:** Early developer of Maker DAO (largest DeFi protocol)
- **Focus:** Key architect of stablecoin systems, blockchain-based decentralized finance
- **Mission:** Wanted to end global banking corruption; advocated for "separation of banking and state"
- **Donation:** Gave $1.4 million to Carnegie Mellon
- **Philosophy:** Believed world would be better if central banks couldn't print money and finance wars

### Mental Health & Behavior
- **Description by Sources:**
  - "Very, very smart" but suffered from extreme bouts of paranoia
  - Saw psychiatrist at times
  - Smoked "a tremendous amount" of pot
  - Had no filter; said exactly what was on his mind
  - Autistic traits: "cocky and arrogant but that was just his autism"
  - Loner who lived alone with dog Sunny after bad breakup
- **Possessions:** Collection of guns (long guns, handguns, rifles) - knew he wanted to protect himself
- **Recent State:** In downward spiral weeks before death; father came to stay with him
- **Recent Status:** Parents said he had been sober for past 6 months

### Terry Davis Influence
Mushegian referenced Terry A. Davis ("God's Lonely Programmer") frequently:
- Davis created Temple OS operating system alone
- Had small cult following but suffered from schizophrenia
- Died August 2018, struck by train (considered suicide)
- Mushegian wrote about Davis: "the people who wanted to create harmful software on purpose caused him to harm himself"

---

## Conspiracy Theories

### His Claims
- September tweet outlined "3 possible futures":
  1. "Suicided by CIA"
  2. "CIA brain damage slave asset"
  3. "Worst nightmare of people who f--ked with me"
- Believed he was fighting "evil" people in "central banking cartel"
- Claimed they used "debt and blackmail" as weapons
- Some paranoia "was based on fact" - he would "discover things" and "knew things"

### Community Reactions
- **Ameen Soleimani (Spankchain CEO):** "My money is on him being murdered. He was found drowned 4 hours after his riskiest tweet ever."
- **Smoky Bear:** "Pedo elites are real... why Epstein suicided himself"
- **Michael Terpin (crypto investor):** "I don't see any way this could have been an accident."
- **Friend:** Feared suicide - "How can I bring some nobility and meaning to my death?"

---

## Official Perspectives

### Brock Pierce (Crypto Billionaire)
- Knew Mushegian; called him "an incredible visionary" and "brilliant"
- Initially confused, but after speaking with family believes it was not murder
- Hinted at self-inflicted death
- His mother clarified death "had nothing to do with his conspiracy tweets"
- Said Mushegian may have misjudged surf and been swept into waves accidentally

### Brock Pierce on Conspiracy Theories
> "Intelligence agencies are not hunting down crypto pioneers. If the government were knocking off people in this field, I would know."

---

## Possible Explanations

1. **Murder (conspiracy):** Found dead hours after specific threat tweet; location with sex trafficking claims
2. **Accidental drowning:** Misjudged rough surf at beach with known riptides
3. **Suicide:** Downward spiral, reclusive behavior, tweet as "bringing significance to death"

**Status:** Unresolved; investigation ongoing
