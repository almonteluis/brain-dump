# Universal Patterns

Structures that appear across seemingly different domains, suggesting underlying principles that govern multiple systems.

## The Energy Spectrum Pattern

**Sequence:** Red → Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple

Appears across:
- **Physics:** Electromagnetic spectrum (red = longest wavelength, violet = shortest)
- **Spirituality:** Chakra system (root = red, crown = violet)
- **Psychology:** Maslow's hierarchy (survival needs = red, self-actualization = purple)
- **Nature:** Rainbow color sequence

## The Core Insight

These aren't separate systems — they're **blueprints of the same universal truth**.

## The Foundation Principle

> Purple is most powerful frequency, BUT red is most vital foundation.

**You cannot fully benefit from the top without securing the bottom first.**

| Domain | Foundation (Red) | Transcendence (Purple) |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|
| Physics | Longest wavelength | Shortest wavelength |
| Chakras | Root (survival) | Crown (enlightenment) |
| Psychology | Safety, belonging | Self-actualization |
| Career | Skills, network | Leadership, impact |

## Practical Application

**When you're stuck, ask:**
- What foundational work am I avoiding?
- Am I chasing purple without securing red?
- Where am I skipping the process?

**The answer:** Work on the foundation, not harder at the outcome.

## Cross-Domain Truth

> "You have to work on the bottom and focus on the basic things if you want to reach the top."

This applies universally:
- Career (fundamentals → mastery)
- Skills (basics → expertise)
- Spirituality (grounding → transcendence)
- Relationships (trust → vulnerability)
