# Agentic AI Maturity

Framework for understanding where organizations are on the journey from having AI to using it strategically.

## The Gap

Most companies (~90%) use AI in at least one function, but only a minority successfully scale pilots. The gap isn't having AI — it's **orchestrating** AI.

## Market Projections

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Agent market growth | $7.8B → $52B by 2030 |
| Enterprise apps with agents (2026) | ~40% |
| Companies successfully scaling | Minority |

## Maturity Levels

### 1. Experimentation
- Individual teams build individual solutions
- Local optimization
- No coordination layer
- **Doesn't scale**

### 2. Systems Talking
- Shared data foundations
- Common platforms
- **Where most enterprises are stuck**

### 3. Strategic Integration
- Coordination layers
- Governance frameworks
- Measurement systems
- **Competitive advantage**

## Assessment Framework

Ask teams what success looks like:

| Response Pattern | Gap Type |
|------------------|----------|
| Everyone gives different answers | **Coordination gap** |
| Can't connect metrics to business outcomes | **Orchestration gap** |
| Can't explain AI decisions' effects | **Governance/Auditability gap** |

## The OOAG Framework

**O**rchestration — **O**bservability — **A**uditability — **G**overnance

Winners in 2026 will be defined by who masters OOAG, not who has the most AI.

## Action Items

1. Prioritize orchestration infrastructure over point solutions
2. Treat governance as product decisions, not compliance
3. Build observability in from the start
4. Design for auditability
5. Shift from centralized IT to empowered line-of-business
