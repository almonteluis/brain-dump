# WCAG 2.1

**Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.1** — published by W3C in June 2018. Defines requirements for making web content accessible to people with disabilities. Builds on WCAG 2.0 with 17 additional success criteria addressing mobile accessibility, low vision, and cognitive/learning disabilities.

## Conformance Levels

| Level | Bar | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Minimum | Alt text, keyboard navigation, no time limits on essential interactions |
| **AA** | Recommended for most sites | Color contrast 4.5:1, captions, resize 200%, error identification |
| **AAA** | Highest | Color contrast 7:1, sign language interpretation, no flashing |

## WCAG 2.1 Adds (vs 2.0)

- Reflow (mobile-friendly layouts)
- Non-text contrast (UI controls + graphics)
- Text spacing (user customization)
- Hover/focus content (accessible tooltips)
- Pointer gestures (no required swipe-only paths)
- Motion actuation (alternatives to shake/tilt)

## Why Marvin Targets WCAG 2.1 AA

- **Federal funding requirement** — federally-funded schools require WCAG 2.1 AA for procurement
- Broader student access — different abilities benefit from AA-level accommodations
- Tracked under epic [PP-304](https://newclassrooms.atlassian.net/browse/PP-304)
