# Meditation Practice

Daily silence practice for mental preparation and clarity. Based on Kobe Bryant's routine and sports psychology research.

## Kobe's Routine

- **Morning:** 15 minutes silence, let thoughts wash over
- **Before games:** 15–20 minutes, visualize obstacles and rehearse responses
- **Purpose:** Anchor for the day. Without it, "constantly chasing the day" instead of controlling it.

## Key Insights

### Stillness vs Motion

| | Sitting in Silence | Quiet Walk |
|---|---|---|
| Brain mode | Restorative | Reactive |
| Why | No external stimuli to respond to | Motion requires environmental awareness |

### Type I vs Type II Fun

From Dr. Jonathan Jenkins (clinical/sports psychologist):

- **Type I fun** — enjoyable throughout (beach, dinner with friends)
- **Type II fun** — hard in moment, satisfying after (tough workout, meditation)

Meditation is Type II fun.

### Physiological Effects

- Duke researcher Imke Kirste: 2 hours daily silence stimulates new hippocampus cell growth (memory/learning region)
- Brief silence lowers cortisol, sharpens focus
- Elite performers (Bryant, Djokovic) use quiet reflection for quick decision-making

## Application

Visualize responses to daily scenarios:
- Difficult interactions at work
- Parenting challenges
- Setting daily intentions
