# AI Coordination Gap

The organizational gap that occurs when multiple AI systems work in isolation without orchestration. Having agents is different from coordinating them.

## The Problem Pattern

A client had 17 different AI implementations across departments:
- Marketing automation
- Supply chain optimization
- HR screening
- Customer service chatbot
- Product recommendations
- Engineering code reviews
- Testing automation

**Each worked fine in isolation.** But when launching cross-functional features, the lack of coordination revealed:
- Existential risks nobody could see
- Blindsides from unconnected systems
- Inability to explain AI decisions across departments

**Nobody had designed these systems to work together.**

## The Three Gaps

| Gap Type | Symptom | Assessment Question |
|----------|---------|---------------------|
| **Coordination** | Everyone gives different answers for AI success | What does success look like? |
| **Orchestration** | Metrics don't connect to business outcomes | How does this create business value? |
| **Governance/Auditability** | Can't explain AI decisions' effects | How do AI decisions affect other systems? |

## Root Cause

Most AI initiatives start with local optimization:
- Individual teams building individual solutions
- No coordination layer
- No shared data foundation
- No common governance framework

This works for **experimentation** but doesn't **scale**.

## The Connection to Process Over Outcome

The AI Coordination Gap is [[process-over-outcome]] at the organizational level:

- Local optimization without coordination = skipping the "interview process" between systems
- Orchestration is the "process" that creates fit between systems
- The outcome (integrated AI) requires the foundation (orchestration infrastructure)

## Performance Impact

MIT research on AI maturity vs financial performance:

| Maturity Level | Performance |
|----------------|-------------|
| Early AI adoption | Below industry average |
| Advanced AI maturity | Well above average |

**The difference isn't having more AI** — it's having the capabilities to use it strategically.

## The Fix

Orchestration means **strategic integration**, not just APIs:
- Systems understand context across domains
- Coordination layer manages specialized components
- Objectives are aligned across teams
- Governance is built in, not bolted on
