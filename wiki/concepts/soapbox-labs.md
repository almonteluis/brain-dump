# SoapBox Labs

Company specializing in speech recognition technology designed specifically for children's voices. Provides voice engines for educational apps, toys, and games.

## Key Characteristics

- Speech recognition optimized for children's voices
- Supports multiple accents and dialects
- Privacy-focused (COPPA/GDPR compliant)
- Real-time audio processing
- Scoring and gamification APIs
