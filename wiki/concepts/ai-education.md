# AI in Education

The transformation of education in the AI era — from initial bans to embracing AI as a tool for learning.

## The Trajectory

**2023 Pattern:**
- **February:** Schools block ChatGPT
- **March:** Schools unblock it
- **Now:** Embracing AI as part of curriculum

The thinking evolved from panic to integration.

## What Works

| Use Case | Effectiveness |
|----------|---------------|
| Quiz variations | Excellent |
| Instruction clarity check | Excellent |
| Brainstorming | Excellent |
| Essay evaluation | Poor |

**Critical insight:** AI great for ideation, poor for evaluation.

## The Detection Reality

> "The short answer is no. The long answer is A.I. use is undetectable."
> — Ethan Mollick, Wharton School

**Implication:** Every instinct about stopping plagiarism doesn't work. The old homework assignment is "basically cracked by A.I."

## The Assessment Question

> "As a teacher, if I'm asking questions that are easily answered by A.I., am I asking the best questions?"

AI as quality filter: If ChatGPT can answer it, maybe it's not worth asking.

## The New Digital Divide

> "I foresee an ever yawning gap between the privileged and everyone else, between those who know how to use A.I. as a tool and those who don't know that there is anything to know."
> — Ricardo Galliano Court, Northwestern University

**The divide:** Not access to computers, but **know-how to use AI effectively**.

## Critical AI Literacy

From Nicole Haddad (SMU):

**Before using AI:**
- What's worth knowing?
- How do I look it up?
- What can I outsource?

**After using AI:**
- What might be missing?
- What can be improved?
- How do I expand the conversation?

## Student Rights

Students should ask:
- Can AI help generate ideas?
- Can AI provide an outline to work on?
- Can AI give feedback on my work?
- Can AI be my teammate?
- Can AI explain why I got something wrong?

## Key Insights

1. Banning failed — adaptation necessary
2. AI detection is impossible
3. Assessment must evolve
4. Literacy over access
5. Equity risk is real
