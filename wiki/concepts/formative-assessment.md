# Formative Assessment

Formative assessment is assessment *for* learning — continuous evidence-gathering about what students understand, with feedback that directly shapes the next instructional move. Not the test at the end of the unit (that's summative). The ongoing, low-stakes, embedded-in-instruction process that tells you "they're getting it" or "they're not, and here's where they're losing the thread."

## The Foundational Numbers

### Black & Wiliam (1998) — *Inside the Black Box*
The seminal meta-analysis. Formative assessment practices significantly improve learning outcomes across age groups, subjects, and contexts. The "black box" metaphor: what happens between teaching input and learning output is where formative assessment operates. Not before. Not after. During.

### Hattie (2009) — *Visible Learning*
From 800+ meta-analyses, formative-related interventions rank at the top:

| Intervention | Effect Size (d) | What that means |
|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Formative evaluation** | 0.90 | Very high impact |
| **Feedback** | 0.73 | High impact |
| **Mastery learning** | 0.57 | High impact |

CBL systems that combine all three — continuous evaluation, rich feedback, mastery-gated progression — are leveraging the most powerful evidence-based interventions simultaneously. Not one at a time. Together.

## Formative vs. Summative

| Dimension | Formative | Summative |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Purpose** | Improve learning during instruction | Evaluate learning after instruction |
| **Timing** | Continuous, embedded | End-of-unit, end-of-term |
| **Stakes** | Low — mistakes are learning data | High — scores count toward grades |
| **Feedback** | Immediate, specific, actionable | Delayed, aggregate |
| **Student role** | Active participant in own assessment | Passive recipient of judgment |

## In CBL Contexts

In [[competency based learning]], formative assessment *replaces* summative events entirely:
- **Continuous low-stakes demonstration** — students repeatedly show competency through performance tasks
- **Mastery-gated progression** — advancement requires demonstrated mastery, creating natural checkpoints
- **Multiple evidence types** — portfolios, teacher observation, self-reflection, AI analysis all contribute
- **Transparent rubrics** — students know mastery criteria before attempting

## The Feedback Connection

Formative assessment generates the evidence. [[adaptive-feedback-education]] determines what to say about it. The strongest systems couple them:
1. Formative assessment identifies where the student is
2. Process-level feedback targets the specific step where they're struggling
3. Motivational feedback sustains engagement through [[productive-struggle]]
4. The loop repeats, refining the model each cycle

The open thread: formative assessment works when teachers have time to act on the evidence. Most teachers don't. They collect the data and then... have 30 other students. This is where AI-assisted analysis and [[knowledge-tracing]] become infrastructure, not luxury — automated evidence collection and interpretation that gives the teacher actionable signals without requiring them to grade 30 formative assessments every night.
