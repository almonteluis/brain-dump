# AI as Management Skill

The core insight that effective AI integration isn't a tool skill — it's a management skill. People who work well with AI aren't just better at prompting; they're better at the senior-level capabilities required to direct complex work.

## The Six Critical Skills

### 1. Context Assembly

Knowing **what information to provide**, from **which sources**, and **why**.

- AI output quality is highly sensitive to input context
- Garbage in, garbage out applies to reasoning, not just data
- Skill: Curating and presenting relevant context

### 2. Quality Judgment

Knowing **when to trust AI output** and **how to verify it**.

- Which parts are reliable?
- What might be hallucinated?
- How to cross-check critical claims?

### 3. Task Decomposition

Breaking problems into **manageable chunks** for AI delegation.

- **Anti-pattern**: Throwing entire projects at AI
- **Pattern**: Chunk → Delegate → Orchestrate → Quantify

### 4. Iterative Refinement

Treating AI output as a **starting point**, not final product.

- Mindset shift: First output is a draft to be refined
- Continuous improvement through feedback loops

### 5. Workflow Integration

Treating AI as **integrated capability**, not side tool.

- The difference between occasional use and systemic leverage
- Building AI into standard operating procedures

### 6. Frontier Recognition

Knowing when you're **operating outside AI capabilities**.

- Recognizing the boundaries of what AI can and cannot do
- Avoiding over-reliance in domains where AI fails

## The Management Equation

```
Effective AI Use = Context + Judgment + Decomposition + Iteration + Integration + Boundary Awareness
```

## Connection to AI Literacy

This concept is the foundation of [[critical-ai-literacy]] — the ability to:

- Discern what's worth knowing
- Figure out how to look it up
- Identify what's worth "outsourcing" to AI
- Think critically about the data
- What might be missing?
- What can be improved?

## The "Tinkerer" Strategy

**Hire tinkerers**: People who explore edges and teach others

**Empower them to**:
- Roam the organization
- Identify automatable problems
- Fix friction points

**Give them**: Ambition, purpose, hard targets

**Watch them**: Fly.

## The Digital Divide Connection

This parallels [[new-ai-divide-tool-literacy-not-access]] — the people who integrate well with AI aren't just good at prompting, they're good at specific senior-level skills like context assembly and quality judgment.

**The new privilege**: Having someone teach you these skills.
