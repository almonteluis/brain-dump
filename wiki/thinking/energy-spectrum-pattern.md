# Energy Spectrum Pattern

> "There's so much that our eyes can't see but our energy can clearly feel... I think I just discovered the most crazy thing about the universe."

Same color sequence everywhere: **Red → Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple**. Rainbows. The electromagnetic spectrum. Chakras. Maslow's hierarchy. Vibrational energy. These aren't separate systems — they're the same blueprint showing up in different languages.

## Physics Foundation

### The Light Spectrum
- **Violet**: shortest wavelength, highest frequency
- **Red**: longest wavelength, lowest frequency
- **Purple is the limit of visible energy** — beyond is ultraviolet, invisible to the naked eye

Purple dye was historically rare and expensive → became the color of royalty. Purple represents wisdom, spirituality, divine connection. The UV pens from childhood — "invisible" ink that's really just beyond what your eyes can process.

## Chakra System

| Chakra | Color | Represents | Frequency |
|--------|------|------------|-----------|
| Root | Red | Survival, trust, grounding | Lowest |
| Sacral | Orange | Creativity, reproduction | Low |
| Solar Plexus | Yellow | Personal power, identity | Low-mid |
| Heart | Green | Love, compassion, healing | Mid |
| Throat | Blue | Communication, truth | High-mid |
| Third Eye | Indigo | Intuition, vision | High |
| Crown | Violet | Unity with divine, enlightenment | Highest |

**Same direction as physics** — lower frequency at the bottom, higher at the top. Root → Crown maps to Red → Violet maps to survival → transcendence.

## Psychology: Maslow's Hierarchy

| Level | Need | Color Parallel |
|-------|------|---------------|
| Physiological | Food, water, shelter | Red (survival, root) |
| Safety | Security, stability | Orange (sacral stability) |
| Love/belonging | Relationships, community | Green (heart, compassion) |
| Esteem | Respect, achievement | Yellow (personal power) |
| Self-actualization | Full potential | Violet (crown, transcendence) |

Maslow didn't use colors. But the sequence maps.

## The Core Decision: Foundation Before Elevation

> "You have to work on the bottom and focus on the basic things if you want to reach the top."

Can't access the crown when the root is blocked. Can't self-actualize when you're hungry. Can't master skills without fundamentals. The pattern says: build from the bottom up, every time.

### When Stuck in the Red Zone

Not feeling safe. Guilt and shame spirals. "I'm not good enough" loops. The fix isn't to push harder toward purple — it's to stabilize red. Feel the guilt, learn from it, move forward. Don't camp there.

The open thread: this pattern is seductive because it's *so* clean. Red→Purple maps everything. But clean patterns can be wrong — they smooth over the messy cases where domains don't align. Not every hierarchy is a progression. Sometimes you need to jump to purple before fixing red (crisis creativity, spiritual awakening from rock bottom). The pattern describes the *default* path, not the only one.
