# AGI Timeline 2026

Working assumption: AGI lands somewhere in 2027-2028. A small community understands the implications. Mainstream isn't taking it seriously. There's a brief window to position before mass awareness kicks in.

## The Positioning Play

| Maximize | Why |
|----------|-----|
| **Mobility** — passport/citizenship flexibility | Geopolitical uncertainty |
| **Resources** — hard currency reserves | Pre-AGI extraction buffer |
| **Adaptability** — embrace uncertainty directly | Mental flexibility for rapid change |

### What Won't Matter

- **Job titles** — AGI outperforms humans in most roles
- **Money** — extract value pre-AGI, but don't delude yourself about long-term meaning

### What Will Matter

Human qualities that transcend economic value:
- Relationships and community
- Physical health and sports
- Caring for others
- Moral and ethical reasoning
- Sound policy benefiting the majority

## The First Turning Philosophy

> "If it cannot be done safely then it should not be built. Artificial super intelligence is a choice, not a guarantee."

The default path doesn't lead to good outcomes. Time is short. Those who understand consequences can push toward better outcomes through belief + action.

### Influence Strategy

1. **Get close to decision-makers** — not necessarily politics; defense tech and adjacent industries matter enormously
2. **Educate and advocate** — increase probability of peaceful, beneficial AGI diffusion
3. **Build in public** — transparency reduces fear, fear produces bad policy

## Personal Implications

The honest read: I'm not positioned for AGI. Nobody is. But I can:
- Build technical depth in AI safety / alignment-adjacent work
- Maintain the TTO/education work as a domain where human qualities matter regardless of AGI timeline
- Stay liquid, stay mobile, stay adaptable

The open thread: the "maximize adaptability" framing sounds right but is strategically empty — adaptable to *what*? The AGI scenario space is too wide for generic adaptability to be useful. The real question is which 2-3 specific scenarios are most likely and what positioning serves all of them.
