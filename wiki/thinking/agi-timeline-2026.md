# AGI Timeline 2026

## Core Prediction

**Timeline**: AGI likely within 1-2 years (by 2027-2028)

**Current Reality Gap**:
- Only a small community understands implications
- Mainstream not taking AGI seriously
- Brief window exists to position before mass awareness

## Strategic Positioning

### What to Maximize Now

| Category | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Mobility** | Passport/citizenship flexibility | Geopolitical uncertainty |
| **Resources** | Hard currency reserves | Pre-AGI extraction buffer |
| **Adaptability** | "Retard maxx" — embrace uncertainty | Mental flexibility for rapid change |

### What Won't Matter

- **Job titles** — AGI will outperform humans in most roles
- **Money** — Extract value pre-AGI, but don't delude yourself about its long-term meaning

### What Will Matter

Human qualities that transcend economic value:
- Relationships and community
- Physical health and sports
- Caring for others
- Moral and ethical reasoning
- Sound policy benefiting the majority

## The First Turning Philosophy

> "If it cannot be done safely then it should not be built. Artificial super intelligence is a choice, not a guarantee."

**The Default Path**: Unlikely to lead to good outcomes. Time is short.

**The Choice**: Those who understand consequences can push toward better outcomes through belief + action.

### Influence Strategy

1. **Get close to decision-makers** — Not necessarily politics; defense tech and adjacent industries matter enormously
2. **Educate and advocate** — Increase probability of peaceful, beneficial AGI diffusion
3. **Act now** — The window for influence is closing

## Open Questions

- What specific skills will remain valuable post-AGI?
- How do we build resilient communities?
- What infrastructure should we prioritize?
