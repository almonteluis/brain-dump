# Andy Matuschak

**Andy Matuschak** is an independent researcher and software engineer working on **tools for thought** and the design of better learning environments. He is best known for his collaborations with [[Michael Nielsen]] on the **mnemonic medium** (Quantum Country, "How Can We Develop Transformative Tools for Thought?") and for his prolific public note-taking practice via his [Working Notes](https://notes.andymatuschak.org/).

## Key Work

- **[Quantum Country](https://quantum.country/)** — co-authored with Michael Nielsen. A long-form essay on quantum computing that embeds spaced-repetition prompts directly into the prose. Canonical example of the mnemonic medium.
- **["How Can We Develop Transformative Tools for Thought?"](https://numinous.productions/ttft/)** — co-authored with Nielsen. Treatise on what would have to be true for tools for thought to actually transform cognition.
- **Working Notes** — public, evergreen, deeply-linked notes practice. Frequently cited as a model for personal knowledge management.
- Prior: led iOS at Khan Academy (years before launching Anki-style mnemonic medium work).

## Why He Matters Here

- Mnemonic medium critiques the same de-contextualized-review failure mode that [[insight-recall|Insight Recall]] critiques, but proposes a different solution (embed prompts in prose vs. surface insights from learner's own history)
- Working Notes practice shapes how to think about [[knowledge-graphs|personal knowledge graphs]] in production
- Cited in [[research-terms-learning-science]] as a researcher worth following directly — citation trails are gold
