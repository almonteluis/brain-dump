# Gergely Orosz

Author of The Pragmatic Engineer newsletter. Former engineer at Uber, Skype, Microsoft. The guy who writes the deep-dives explaining how tools like Cursor and Claude Code are actually built — not the marketing version, the engineering version.

His "real-world engineering challenges" series is the most honest coverage of AI dev tools in the industry. He talks to the teams building them, publishes what he learns, and doesn't sugarcoat the limitations. That's rare in a space full of hype.

## Notable Work

- **The Pragmatic Engineer** — Newsletter and podcast on software engineering. Consistently good.
- **Real-world engineering challenges series** — Deep-dives into Cursor, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Anthropic Artifacts. The source for [[how-claude-code-is-built]].
- **Engineering career guides** — Books on software engineering careers.
