# Andrej Karpathy

**Source:** Andrej Karpathy.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-29
**Type:** Biography

## Summary

Slovak-Canadian AI researcher. OpenAI co-founder, former Tesla AI director, founder of Eureka Labs (AI education). Coined "vibe coding."

## Key Facts

- **Born:** 23 October 1986, Bratislava, Czechoslovakia (now Slovakia)
- **Education:**
  - BS Computer Science + Physics, University of Toronto (2009)
  - MS, University of British Columbia (2011) — physically simulated figures
  - PhD Stanford (2015) — NLP + computer vision, advisor Fei-Fei Li
- **CS 231n:** Created first deep learning course at Stanford. Grew from 150 students (2015) to 750 (2017)
- **OpenAI:** Founding member, research scientist 2015-2017. Returned Feb 2023, left Feb 2024
- **Tesla:** Director of AI and Autopilot Vision, June 2017 - July 2022. Reported to Elon Musk
- **Eureka Labs:** Founded July 2024. AI education platform. First course: LLM101n
- **YouTube:** "Zero to Hero" neural network series, "How I use LLMs"
- **Time 100 Most Influential People in AI** (2024)
- **Coined "vibe coding"** (Feb 2025)
