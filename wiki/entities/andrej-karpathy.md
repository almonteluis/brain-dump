# Andrej Karpathy

Slovak-Canadian AI researcher. OpenAI co-founder, former Tesla AI director, founder of Eureka Labs (AI education). The guy who built CS 231n — the Stanford deep learning course that went from 150 students to 750 in two years and became the canonical introduction to computer vision for a whole generation of engineers. Coined "vibe coding" in February 2025, which is either his most or least consequential contribution depending on how the next decade goes.

## The Facts

- **Born:** 23 October 1986, Bratislava, Czechoslovakia (now Slovakia)
- **Education:** BS CS + Physics (Toronto, 2009) → MS (UBC, 2011) → PhD (Stanford, 2015, advisor Fei-Fei Li)
- **CS 231n:** Created Stanford's first deep learning course. 150 students (2015) → 750 (2017). Still the gold standard.
- **OpenAI:** Founding member 2015-2017. Returned Feb 2023, left Feb 2024.
- **Tesla:** Director of AI and Autopilot Vision, June 2017 - July 2022. Reported to Elon Musk.
- **Eureka Labs:** Founded July 2024. AI education platform. First course: LLM101n.
- **Time 100 Most Influential People in AI** (2024)

## Why He Matters Here

Karpathy occupies a weird intersection: he's a serious researcher who also has a talent for *teaching*. The "Zero to Hero" neural network series on YouTube, the blog posts, the courses — he builds the on-ramps that let other people enter the field. Eureka Labs is the formalization of that instinct: what if AI could scale the kind of teaching that CS 231n provided?

That question rhymes with the TTO/[[new-classrooms]] question. The difference: Karpathy is optimizing for motivated adult learners choosing to engage. TTO has to work for every kid in the room, including the ones who'd rather be anywhere else.
