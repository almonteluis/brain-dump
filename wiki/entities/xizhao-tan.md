# Xizhao Tan

Xizhao Tan co-authored the 2025 IRec paper with [[xuefei-hou]] — **"A Metacognitive Scaffolding for Self-Regulated Learning through Just-in-Time Insight Recall"** (arXiv:2506.20156). The paper introduces the [[insight-recall]] paradigm and the IRec prototype system.

## Key Contribution

- Co-authored the conceptual + system paper formalizing Insight Recall as a [[just-in-time-adaptive-intervention|JITAI-framed]] metacognitive scaffold
- Proposed the three-path hybrid retrieval engine + LLM similarity assessment as the core technical mechanism

The paper is notable because it doesn't just propose the idea — it builds a working prototype with three retrieval strategies (exact, semantic, LLM-mediated) and evaluates it. Most learning science papers stop at the framework. This one ships code.
