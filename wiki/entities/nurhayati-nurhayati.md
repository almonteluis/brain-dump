# Nurhayati Nurhayati

Researcher at **Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta** in Indonesia. Lead author of the 2025 PRISMA systematic review **"Gamification in School Education"** (AL-ISHLAH: Jurnal Pendidikan, Vol 17 No 2). The review synthesized 21 peer-reviewed studies (2021–2024) on gamification in K-12 education — what elements work, under what conditions, and where the evidence breaks down.

## Key Contribution

Identified the most effective gamification elements: points, badges, leaderboards, progress tracking, real-time feedback, collaboration, real-world applications, autonomy support. Also mapped boundary conditions — what works in elementary doesn't necessarily transfer to high school, and technology infrastructure constraints matter more than most researchers acknowledge.
