# Carol Ann Tomlinson

Carol Ann Tomlinson developed and popularized the framework of **differentiated instruction**. *The Differentiated Classroom* (1999) is the standard reference for how teachers adapt instruction to meet diverse needs within the same room. Her core distinction: differentiation is proactive planning for differences, not reactive remediation after kids fall behind.

## Key Works

### *The Differentiated Classroom* (1999)

The foundational text:

- **Three student factors**: readiness, interest, learning profile
- **Four differentiation levers**: content, process, product, learning environment
- **Key principle**: differentiation is proactive planning for differences, not reactive remediation

### *Leading and Managing a Differentiated Classroom* (Tomlinson & Imbeau, 2010)

Critical clarification: differentiation is **not** individualized instruction (a unique plan for every student). It's flexible grouping and responsive teaching. That distinction matters for scalability — you can differentiate for 30 kids. You can't individualize for 30 kids.

## Core Ideas

1. **Fairness ≠ sameness** — fair means every student gets what they need to grow
2. **Assessment drives instruction** — ongoing assessment of readiness is the foundation
3. **Flexibility is essential** — adjust grouping, pacing, and approaches based on evidence
4. **Student voice matters** — students should have agency in how they learn and demonstrate learning

## Relevance to CBL and TTO

Tomlinson's framework maps directly to what Teach to One does:

- **Readiness** → demonstrated competency (measured by [[knowledge-tracing]], not age/grade)
- **Content differentiation** → algorithmic content selection based on skill mastery
- **Process differentiation** → multiple instructional modalities (teacher-led, collaborative, independent, AI-tutored)
- **Pacing differentiation** → mastery-gated advancement (the core CBL mechanism)
- **Environment differentiation** → flexible grouping in station-rotation models

TTO is Tomlinson's framework operationalized by algorithm. The question Tomlinson answers theoretically ("what would it look like to differentiate for every kid?") TTO answers practically ("here's the daily schedule").
