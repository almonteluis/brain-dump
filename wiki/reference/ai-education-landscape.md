# AI Education Landscape

## The Shift: Bans to Embracing

Walla Walla High School trajectory:
- **February**: Blocked student access to ChatGPT
- **March**: Unblocked it
- **Now**: Embracing AI tools as part of curriculum

> "Now that the first full school year of the A.I. chatbot era is beginning... their thinking had evolved"

This pattern repeated across districts nationwide — initial panic giving way to integration.

---

## Teacher Use Cases That Work

| Use Case | Effectiveness |
|----------|---------------|
| **Quiz variations** | Excellent - generates multiple versions |
| **Instruction clarity check** | Excellent - catches ambiguities |
| **Brainstorming activities** | Excellent - ideation partner |
| **Essay evaluation** | Poor - "isn't great at that" |

**Critical insight**: Teachers learning what AI is good at vs where it falls short.

---

## The Best Practice: Critical AI Literacy

Nicole Haddad (Southern Methodist University) approach:

**Before ChatGPT:**
- Teach students to discern what's worth knowing
- Figure out how to look it up
- Identify what's worth "outsourcing" to AI

**After using AI:**
- Think critically about the data
- What might be missing?
- What can be improved?
- How to expand the "conversation" for richer feedback

This is [[ai-as-management-skill|AI as Management Skill]] applied to education — knowing when to use AI and how to verify its output.

## Student Experiences

### Benefits
- **Personalized tutoring**: "Ask it a million questions and it never gets tired" — Amedeo, 13
- **Simplified concepts**: "Understand difficult concepts in a simple way"
- **Endless examples**: Outlines for essays, business plans, emails
- **Time saver**: Real efficiency gain

### Concerns
- **Industrialized plagiarism**: "They have industrialized and automated plagiarism" — Travis Huckell
- **Loss of discovery**: "The joy of learning is that 'AHA!' moment that comes from figuring something out yourself" — Emma Nazario
- **Lazy thinking**: "You don't have to think about a text deeply"

## The Equity Gap

> "I foresee an ever yawning gap between the privileged and everyone else, between those who know how to use A.I. as a tool and those who don't know that there is anything to know."
> — Ricardo Galliano Court, Northwestern University

**The new digital divide**: Not access to computers, but know-how to use AI effectively.

---

## Detection Reality

> "The short answer is no. The long answer is A.I. use is undetectable. You can't ask A.I. to detect A.I. It's just going to lie to you."
> — Ethan Mollick, Wharton School

**Implication**: Every instinct about stopping plagiarism doesn't work. The old homework assignment is "basically cracked by A.I."

---

## The Assessment Question

> "As a teacher, if I'm asking questions that are easily answered by A.I., am I asking the best questions?"
> — Jennifer Parnell, history teacher

**AI as quality filter**: If ChatGPT can answer it, maybe it's not worth asking.

Jennifer Parnell's approach:
- Had ChatGPT manufacture an essay
- Students analyzed the AI-generated text for errors and rewrote it
- Students fed their own essays to AI for feedback on source quality

---

## What Students Should Demand

Ethan Mollick's advice for students:
- Ask for clarity on what's allowed
- Can AI help generate ideas?
- Can AI provide an outline I work on?
- Can AI give feedback on my work?
- Can AI be my teammate?
- Can AI explain why I got something wrong?

> "You are allowed as a student to ask for what does this mean, while being patient with your teachers that they haven't figured it out either. Nobody knows the answer."

---

## Key Insights

1. **Banning failed**: Schools that blocked AI are now unblocking and embracing
2. **Use cases matter**: AI great for ideation, poor for evaluation
3. **Literacy over access**: Teaching critical AI usage more important than the tool itself
4. **Detection is impossible**: Stop trying to catch, start adapting
5. **Assessment must evolve**: Questions AI can answer are questions worth rethinking
6. **Equity risk**: New divide between those who know how to use AI vs those who don't
