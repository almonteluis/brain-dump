# Back Button Hijacking Spam Policy

**Source:** [[Introducing a new spam policy for "back button hijacking"    Google Search Central Blog]]
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-29
**Type:** policy announcement

## Summary

Google expanding spam policies to explicitly prohibit back button hijacking — interfering with browser navigation to prevent users from returning to previous pages. Enforcement begins June 15, 2026.

## Key Claims

1. **Back button hijacking** = interfering with browser history so back button doesn't return to previous page. Users sent to unsolicited pages/ads or prevented from normal browsing.
2. **Explicit violation** of "malicious practices" spam policy. Pages may receive manual spam actions or automated demotions.
3. **Two-month grace period** — policy published April 12, enforcement June 15, 2026.
4. **Site owner responsibility** — review technical implementation, remove hijacking code even if from third-party libraries or ad platforms.
5. **Reconsideration request** available in Search Console after fixing.
