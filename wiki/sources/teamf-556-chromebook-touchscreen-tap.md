# TEAMF-556: Chromebook Touchscreen Tap Shifts Display Left

Source: [Jira — TEAMF-556](https://newclassrooms.atlassian.net/browse/TEAMF-556)

## Summary

On Chromebook devices, a touchscreen tap causes the display to shift left. Requires zoom in/out to reset. Affects student experience on touchscreen Chromebooks.
