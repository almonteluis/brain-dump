# Agentic AI Maturity Gap

## Summary

The gap between having Agentic AI and knowing what to do with it. The 2026 competitive edge belongs to organizations that master orchestration, observability, and auditability — not just those with the most AI implementations.

## Key Claims

- ~90% of companies use AI in at least one function, but only a minority successfully scale pilots
- Agent market projected to grow from $7.8B → $52B by 2030
- ~40% of enterprise apps will have AI agents by end of 2026
- Having Agents ≠ Orchestrating Agents — coordination is the hard part
- Winners will be defined by who figures out OOAG (Orchestration, Observability, Auditability, Governance)

## Maturity Assessment

Ask teams what success looks like:
- Different answers → **coordination gap**
- Can't connect metrics to business outcomes → **orchestration gap**
- Can't explain AI decisions' effects → **governance/auditability gap**

## Maturity Progression

1. **Experimentation** — Individual solutions (doesn't scale)
2. **Systems talking** — Shared data, common platforms (where most are stuck)
3. **Strategic integration** — Coordination layers, governance frameworks

## Action Items

1. Prioritize orchestration infrastructure over point solutions
2. Treat governance as product decisions, not compliance
3. Build observability in from the start
4. Design for auditability
5. Shift from centralized IT to empowered line-of-business
