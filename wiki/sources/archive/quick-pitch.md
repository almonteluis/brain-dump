I have an idea for the weekly goal tracker on the header to be expanded further than just tracking skill completion. 

The idea is that, since my work with the brain break feature, I had to build a context provider which acts as an orchestrator across different pages and tracking user actions and triggers at particular use cases. Seeing the weekly goal in the header and the work that we are doing in the team E, creating new skill practice experiences for certain skills, had me thinking: Hey, I think around this time the year is coming to a close. Students are tackling more difficult parts of the curriculum, with math, and gearing up for tests. they may not be hitting those goals as often at this point as opposed to earlier in the year.

I want to add two more rings within the weekly goal tracker will be ideal. currently we are tracking two skills completed. I want to include another ring inside which will be dedicated towards practice time.

kids who might be struggling at a particular skill that hasn't been passed the skill challenge to still feel like they are making progress by practicing. This could expand to practice problems and watching work example videos.

The third ring will be the maintenance ring and I was thinking that we can introduce spaced repetition at this ring. We can resurface previous master skills and surface that up to them as kind of in-between skills, like: "Hey you just completed this skill and the next skill that you got up, there's a predecessor skill that you previously completed three months ago." I think this would be a good time to brush up on that skill before moving forward and that will keep that ring in progress. And we also have the student data to recommend and surface That  messaging for the spaced repetition. Already on a data level it should be too difficult. Maybe for the backend team they have to expand it to be like: 'Hey for spaced repetition, include skills that are in their top skills but also are a predecessor or two levels down.' I don't know but it's just a rough idea.
