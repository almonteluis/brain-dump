## Key Philosophies Extracted

### 1. The Universal Pattern
All systems follow the same color sequence:
- **Physics:** Red → Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple
- **Chakras:** Root → Sacral → Solar Plexus → Heart → Throat → Third Eye → Crown
- **Psychology:** Survival → Social → Esteem → Self-actualization
- **Nature:** Rainbow sequence (always the same)

### 2. Foundation Before Elevation
You cannot access higher levels (purple/crown/enlightenment) when the base (red/root) is unhealed.

**In career:** Skip the resume/portfolio/interview process → bad fit
**In life:** Chase enlightenment while neglecting basic needs → burnout

### 3. Process Over Outcome
Life is not about outcomes — it's about the process. The outcome emerges from going through the steps.

### 4. Guilt as Intuition
Guilt isn't shame — it's your soul saying "I expect more from you."

**Process:** Feel it → repent → do better → move on (don't stay stuck)

### 5. Colors as Energy Tools
- **Red:** grounding, courage, vitality
- **Green:** healing, safety, nature
- **Blue:** communication, trust, calm
- **Purple:** spirituality, intuition, meditation
