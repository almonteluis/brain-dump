## Summary

Sunday May 3 voice memo. R&B party plans. Self-paced student motivation research test successful (to integrate into Obsidian vault). Math problem game concept. Teach to One tier system thoughts.

## Key Claims

- Research test on self-paced learning + motivation succeeded — will integrate into main vault.
- **Math Problem Game concept:** Math problems feel arbitrary to students. Good math game should trigger "eureka moment" — the click of pattern recognition just before the answer. Abstraction tools (diagrams) frame worked examples to surface hidden patterns. Game mechanics should target that recognition moment, not just the answer.
- **Teach to One tier system:** Tier 1 = in-class math instruction (regular teacher). Tier 2 = math lab (supplemental, like study hall). US Virgin Islands pilot: one school has high engagement, others don't. Reasons for low engagement: violence/threats, staff not trained, internet connectivity issues.
- Tier 1 and tier 2 teachers are disconnected — no shared curriculum view. Need: per-student graph showing "where they are, what they completed, what they're working toward" — visible to both math teacher and math lab instructor.
- Math lab feels like "study hall with rotating teachers" — should be dedicated Teach to One time with curriculum alignment.
