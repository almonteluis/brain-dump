## Key Frameworks Extracted

### The Governance Formula
```
Orchestration + Observability + Auditability = Governance
```

### Three Types of Gaps
1. **Coordination Gap** — Everyone gives different answers for AI success
2. **Orchestration Gap** — Metrics don't connect to business outcomes
3. **Governance/Auditability Gap** — Can't explain AI decisions' effects

### Decision Velocity Equation
```
Decision Velocity = (Automation + Coordination) × Confidence
```

## Core Insights

1. **The Competitive Shift (2026)** — Winners determined by orchestration, observability, and auditability, not just AI quantity

2. **Governance is Speed** — Companies treating governance as features move 5-7x faster than competitors in manual approval chains

3. **Orchestration ≠ Integration** — Real orchestration means AI systems understand context across domains

4. **The Foundational Question** — "What data can we trust, and what do we need to fix before we automate decisions at scale?"
