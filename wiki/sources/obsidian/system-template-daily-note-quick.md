## Summary

Minimal daily note template for fast tracking. Sections include Today (Done, Learned), Key Insight, Tomorrow priorities, and Read/Watched. Designed for 2-minute completion on busy days.

## Key Claims

- Quick template: Done list, Learned list, one Key Insight, Tomorrow priorities, Read/Watched
- Best for: Busy days, minimal notes preference, quick check-ins
- Consistency over completeness — brief daily entry maintains habit
