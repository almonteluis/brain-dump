# Article Template

**Source:** raw/06_system/templates/article-template.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-09
**Type:** template

## Summary

Comprehensive template for processing articles into the knowledge vault. Includes YAML frontmatter with taxonomy (insights, concepts, frameworks, tools), Universal Mining Framework sections (Layer 1 and Layer 2), connections to vault notes, and quality standards checklist.

## Key Claims

- Taxonomy section tracks: insights, concepts, frameworks, tools_mentioned
- Universal Mining Framework separates explicit (Layer 1) from implicit (Layer 2) content
- Quality standards require: full read, 3-7 insights, explicit and implicit content, frameworks, connections, action inspiration, rating
- Processing checklist ensures complete integration before moving from inbox to sources

- [[layer-1-content]] — Explicit ideas, frameworks, philosophies, decisions
- [[layer-2-content]] — Implicit embedded ideas and philosophies
- [[action-inspiration]] — This week and long-term actionable items
- [[quality-checklist]] — Completion verification standards
