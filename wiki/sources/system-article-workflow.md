# Article Processing Workflow

**Source:** raw/06_system/ARTICLE-WORKFLOW.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-09
**Type:** documentation

## Summary

Complete workflow for capturing, processing, and organizing articles into the knowledge vault. Pipeline: Discover → Capture → Process → Integrate → Retrieve. Uses Universal Mining Framework with Layer 1 (explicit) and Layer 2 (implicit) content extraction.

## Key Claims

- Article processing has 4 stages: Capture (inbox), Process (deep read), Integrate (file & link), Retrieve (use knowledge)
- Quality standards require 3-7 key insights, applicable frameworks, connection points, and action inspiration
- Layer 2 mining extracts embedded ideas, implicit philosophies, and decisions by omission
- Processed articles move from `00_inbox/Links/` to `02_reference/sources/articles/`
