# Research Terms — Learning Science Taxonomy

**Source:** Research terms.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-05-09
**Type:** Reference taxonomy / search-term map

## Summary

Curated research-terms taxonomy organized by how the user would actually use them when searching learning-science literature. Narrow enough to return signal, broad enough to surface adjacent fields. Builds the citation backbone for ongoing ed-tech research thread.

## Student Pace — Core Concepts

- Mastery-based progression
- Self-paced learning (vs competency-based — different traditions, both worth searching)
- Learning velocity / learning gain
- Pacing guides + adaptive curriculum
- Time-on-task vs mastery
- Productive struggle (term teachers actually use)
- Desirable difficulties (cog sci framing of same idea)

## Adaptive Systems and Student Modeling

- [[knowledge-tracing|Knowledge tracing]] (umbrella term — DKT, BKT, SAKT are instances)
- [[Intelligent tutoring systems]] (ITS) — older but foundational
- Learner modeling
- **Open learner models** — subset where the model is shown back to the student. Directly relevant to [[three-ring-mastery-system|the rings]]
- **Stealth assessment** ([[Valerie Shute]]'s work — assessment without student knowing they're being assessed)

## Teacher-Facing

- Teacher dashboards / learning analytics dashboards (LAD)
- **Orchestration tools** (literal term for what teachers do — whole subfield)
- Co-orchestration / human-AI orchestration in classrooms
- Formative assessment + data-informed instruction
- Classroom analytics
- Teacher noticing (teacher-cognition lit — how teachers actually decide who needs help)
- Actionable analytics

## The Pace–Teacher Intersection

- Differentiated instruction at scale
- Small-group instruction + ability grouping (loaded term, search carefully)
- Targeted intervention triggers
- Early warning systems (predictive flags for struggling students)
- **Workload-aware teacher tools** (newer subfield — designed around what teachers actually have time for)

## Theoretical Anchors

- [[self-regulated-learning]] (SRL) + [[metacognition]]
- Cognitive load theory (Sweller)
- Expertise reversal effect (why one-size-fits-all instruction breaks down)
- [[spaced-repetition|Spacing effect]] + interleaving + retrieval practice (the "big three" of learning science)

## Search Patterns That Outperform Keywords

- `[concept] middle school` — filters out higher-ed-only results
- `[concept] systematic review` or `[concept] meta-analysis` — synthesis layer
- `[concept] teacher perspective` or `teacher-facing [concept]` — flips the lens
- `failure modes [concept]` or `unintended consequences [concept]` — counterargument literature
- `[two concepts] AND` — bridge searches like "ZPD AND knowledge tracing" surface integration papers

## Meta-Strategies

1. When you find a paper you like, scroll to its citations and find the most-cited paper in its references. Usually the foundational text in the subfield.
2. For ed-tech specifically, prioritize **2018+** unless hunting foundational theory. Field moves fast; pre-2018 ITS literature often assumes constraints (no smartphones, no cloud LMS) that no longer apply.
