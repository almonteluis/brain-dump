# Cribbs & Utley (2023) — Validated Math Identity Instrument for Grades 5–12

**Source:** [Mathematics Education Research Journal](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13394-023-00474-w)
**Authors:** Juliana Cribbs, Juliana Utley

## Summary

Developed and validated the first efficient, group-administrable mathematics identity instrument spanning grades 5 through 12. Addresses a significant gap: prior math identity measures lacked validity evidence for efficient group administration across the full grade span. Math identity is conceptualized as a multidimensional construct encompassing students' sense of themselves as math learners and doers. Standard psychometric analyses establish reliability and validity evidence.

## Key Claims

- First validated group-administrable math identity instrument for grades 5–12
- Math identity is multidimensional (sense of self as math learner and doer)
- The instrument is efficient enough for practical use in educational settings
- Prior measures had gaps in validity evidence and grade-span coverage
- Provides a standardized tool for measuring math identity change over time

## Entities Mentioned

- Psychometric validation methods (reliability, validity evidence)
