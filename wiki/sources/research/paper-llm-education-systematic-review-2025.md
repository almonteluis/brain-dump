## One-line signal
A systematic review of 88 empirical studies (Nov 2022–Mar 2025) finds LLMs are being rapidly deployed in education for personalized learning, assessment, and classroom support, but privacy and governance challenges remain unresolved.

## Core ideas
- Reviews exclusively empirical evaluations of LLM-based educational applications — no theoretical/speculative papers
- Key application areas: personalized learning, automated assessment, classroom support, writing feedback, tutoring
- LLMs show measurable improvements in learning outcomes compared to traditional methods in many contexts
- Recurring challenges: data privacy, algorithmic bias, hallucination, over-reliance by students
- Governance frameworks for LLM use in education are still nascent
- Documents a significant shift from theoretical discussions to real-world deployment

## Why this matters for the Three-Ring project
Provides the empirical foundation for integrating LLMs into a student motivation app — confirms that LLM-based interventions in educational settings produce measurable outcomes, while highlighting the privacy and governance considerations we must address for a middle/high school audience.
