## One-line signal
Presents a multi-agent AI platform for personalized and adaptive math tutoring that enables students to learn new topics, identify weaknesses, revise for exams, and practice on unlimited personalized exercises.

## Core ideas
- Multi-agent architecture for math tutoring — different AI agents handle different tutoring functions
- System supports multiple learning modes: learning new topics, targeted weakness practice, exam revision, unlimited exercises
- Personalized exercise generation based on student performance and gaps
- Adaptive difficulty adjustment as students progress
- Designed for scalable deployment in real educational settings

## Why this matters for the Three-Ring project
Demonstrates a multi-agent approach to adaptive math education that Three-Ring could emulate. The ability to identify student weaknesses and generate unlimited personalized practice problems directly supports a motivation-driven app — students stay engaged when content is appropriately challenging and tailored to their needs.
