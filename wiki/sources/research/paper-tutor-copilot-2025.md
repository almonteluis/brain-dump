## One-line signal
Tutor CoPilot is a human-AI system that provides real-time expert-guided suggestions to tutors during math sessions; in an RCT with 700+ tutors and 1,000 students, it increased topic mastery by 4 percentage points overall and 9 p.p. for students of lower-rated tutors — at ~$20/tutor/year.

## Core ideas
- Human-AI approach: AI augments human tutors rather than replacing them — models expert thinking and provides real-time suggestions
- RCT with 700+ tutors and 1,000+ students from underserved communities
- Students whose tutors used Tutor CoPilot were 4 p.p. more likely to master math topics (p<0.01)
- ==Largest gains for students of lower-rated tutors (+9 p.p.) — AI helps bridge skill gaps==
- Analysis of 350,000+ messages shows Tutor CoPilot increases probing questions and reduces generic praise
- Extremely cost-effective at ~$20/tutor/year
- Demonstrates that AI can scale expertise in real-world educational domains

## Why this matters for the Three-Ring project
Strong evidence that AI-assisted tutoring improves math outcomes, especially for struggling students. Three-Ring could incorporate similar real-time coaching suggestions — not replacing teacher interaction but augmenting it. The finding that generic praise is reduced in favor of probing questions is directly relevant to motivation design.
