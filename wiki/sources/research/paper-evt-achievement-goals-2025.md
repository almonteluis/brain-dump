# EVT and Achievement Goals in Math (2025)

Paper applying Expectancy-Value Theory to achievement goal orientations in math. The framework: students pursue math (or don't) based on two judgments — "can I do this?" (expectancy) and "do I care?" (value). Achievement goals layer on top: mastery goals (I want to learn) vs. performance goals (I want to look smart / not look dumb).

[[achievement-goal-theory]] is already in the vault. This paper connects EVT to those goal orientations specifically in math contexts, where [[math-anxiety]] distorts both expectancy ("I can't do math") and value ("math is pointless"). The design implication for [[project-atlas]]: the system can influence expectancy through well-calibrated difficulty (hitting the [[zone-of-proximal-development]]). Influencing value is harder — that's where [[behavioral-nudges-education]] and relevance signaling come in.
