# Generative AI in Mathematics Education: A Meta-Analysis

## Summary

This meta-analysis synthesizes findings from 22 empirical studies (N=5,232) examining the impact of generative AI on mathematics education outcomes. Results indicate a statistically significant positive effect of GenAI on math achievement, with the strongest effects observed for conceptual understanding and reductions in math anxiety. Effects are heterogeneous across studies, and K-12 evidence is still emerging relative to higher education contexts. The analysis identifies key moderating variables including intervention duration, AI modality, and the type of mathematical content targeted.

## Key Claims

- Generative AI has a statistically significant positive effect on mathematics learning outcomes across the reviewed studies.
- The strongest effects are on **conceptual understanding** (not just procedural fluency), suggesting AI tools support deeper mathematical reasoning.
- GenAI interventions reduce **math anxiety**, a critical affective factor that predicts engagement and persistence.
- Effects are heterogeneous — not all AI interventions are equally effective; design and implementation quality matter.
- K-12 evidence is promising but thinner than higher education; more rigorous RCTs are needed in middle school contexts.
