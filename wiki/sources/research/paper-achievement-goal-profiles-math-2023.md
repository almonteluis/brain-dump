# Chan & Liem (2023) — Achievement Goal Profiles in Mathematics

**Source:** [British Journal of Educational Psychology](https://bpspsychub.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/bjep.12620)
**Authors:** Jenny K.Y. Chan, Gregory Arief D. Liem

## Summary

Used single-level and multilevel latent mixture modeling to identify distinct achievement goal profiles among mathematics students. Profiles differ meaningfully in their associations with math achievement, self-efficacy, and anxiety. Multi-goal profiles (students pursuing both mastery and performance goals) show complex patterns that challenge simplistic goal framing. Crucially, class-level instructional quality dimensions (classroom management, supportive climate, instructional clarity, cognitive activation) moderate which goal profiles students adopt — goal profiles are shaped by the instructional environment, not just individual traits.

## Key Claims

- Distinct achievement goal profiles exist (e.g., mastery-oriented, performance-oriented, multi-goal) and predict different outcomes
- Instructional quality at the classroom level moderates profile adoption — students in higher-quality environments are more likely to adopt adaptive profiles
- Background covariates (gender, prior achievement) predict profile membership
- Multi-goal profiles challenge simplistic mastery-vs-performance framing
- Bridging individual motivation with classroom context is essential

## Entities Mentioned

- Achievement goal profiles (mastery-oriented, performance-oriented, multi-goal)
- Instructional quality dimensions: classroom management, supportive climate, instructional clarity, cognitive activation
- Math achievement, self-efficacy, math anxiety as outcome variables
- Latent profile analysis / latent mixture modeling methodology
