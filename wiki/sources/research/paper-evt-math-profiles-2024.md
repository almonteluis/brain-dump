# Mayerhofer, Lüftenegger & Eichmair (2024) — Math EVT Profiles During Secondary-to-Tertiary Transition

**Source:** [International Journal of STEM Education](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40594-024-00491-6)
**Authors:** Mayerhofer, M., Lüftenegger, M., & Eichmair, M.

## Summary

Measured five EVT constructs (intrinsic value, attainment value, utility value, cost, expectancy of success) across two time points during the secondary-to-tertiary transition into STEM fields (T1: n=710, T2: n=487). Using latent profile analysis, identified distinct motivational profiles at each time point and examined profile transitions. Mathematics expectancy-value patterns predicted cognitive effort, modeling engagement, and STEM attitudes. The transition period is a critical window where motivation profiles are unstable — a key intervention opportunity.

## Key Claims

- Math motivation profiles shift significantly during the secondary-to-tertiary transition
- Profile membership is unstable — students move between profiles across the transition
- EVT profiles predict cognitive effort, STEM engagement, and attitudes
- The secondary-to-tertiary transition is a critical intervention window
- Multi-dimensional EVT measurement (expectancy + multiple value dimensions + cost) is more informative than single "motivation scores"

## Entities Mentioned
