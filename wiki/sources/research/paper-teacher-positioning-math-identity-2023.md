# Canonigo & Joaquin (2023) — Teacher Positioning Shapes Math Identity

**Source:** [Cogent Education](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/2331186X.2023.2190310)
**Authors:** Antoniette M. Canonigo, Michail N. B. Joaquin

## Summary

Found that students' perception of teacher positioning — how teachers frame students' roles and capabilities — shapes their mathematics identity. Problem-solving flexibility (students' ability to use multiple strategies and approaches) partially mediates the teacher positioning → math identity pathway. Highlights that math identity is not purely internal but co-constructed through teacher-student interactions and classroom discourse. Connects social positioning theory with cognitive flexibility in math.

## Key Claims

- Teacher positioning significantly shapes math identity
- Problem-solving flexibility partially mediates the positioning → identity pathway
- Math identity is co-constructed through teacher-student interactions
- When teachers position students as capable mathematical thinkers, students develop greater flexibility
- Social positioning theory bridges motivational and cognitive domains
