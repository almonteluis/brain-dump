# Streak Mechanics and Math Engagement

Research on whether the streak mechanics that keep people grinding [[duolingo]] lessons also work for math. The hypothesis is straightforward: streaks exploit loss aversion (you don't want to lose what you've built), goal-gradient effects (closer to a milestone = more effort), and habit formation (daily practice becomes automatic). [[paper-nudge-math-megastudy-2025|The Duckworth megastudy]] found that behavioral nudges move math outcomes. Streaks are the most gamified version of that nudge.

The open question: [[duolingo]] streaks protect a language-learning habit — low cognitive cost, high frequency. Math is harder, less frequent, and more frustrating. Does a streak survive the day the kid hits a wall on fractions? Or does the streak add pressure to an already stressful moment, making it worse?
