## One-line signal
A systematic literature review of knowledge graph construction and application in education, covering methodologies across five key domains including personalized learning, curriculum design, concept mapping, and educational content recommendation.

## Core ideas
- SLR examining knowledge graph (KG) construction methodologies and educational applications
- Five key application domains: personalized learning, curriculum design, concept mapping, educational content recommendation, and learning analytics
- KGs provide structured representations of knowledge relationships essential for adaptive systems
- Construction approaches range from manual ontology engineering to automated extraction from educational materials
- KGs enable intelligent content recommendation by mapping prerequisite relationships between concepts
- Identifies research gaps: scalability of construction methods, integration with real-time learning systems, evaluation metrics

## Why this matters for the Three-Ring project
Knowledge graphs could underpin Three-Ring's skill map — representing math topics as interconnected nodes with prerequisite relationships. This SLR provides the methodological foundation for building a math knowledge graph that enables the app to recommend appropriate next challenges, visualize student progress through connected skills, and identify knowledge gaps.
