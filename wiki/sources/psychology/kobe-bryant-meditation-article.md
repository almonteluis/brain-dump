## Summary

Clipping of The Athletic article on Kobe Bryant's daily meditation practice, with personal annotations and questions. Bryant meditated 15 minutes every morning and 15–20 minutes before games.

## Key Claims

- Kobe: "It sets me up for the rest of the day. It's like having an anchor. If I don't do it, I feel like I'm constantly chasing the day as opposed to being able to be controlled and dictate the day."
- **Type I vs Type II fun** (Dr. Jonathan Jenkins): Type I = enjoyable throughout (beach, dinner). Type II = hard in moment, satisfying after (tough workout, meditation).
- **Sitting in silence vs quiet walk:** In stillness, brain shifts to restorative mode. In motion, brain stays reactive.
- **Hippocampus growth:** Duke researcher Imke Kirste found 2 hours of daily silence stimulates new cell growth in hippocampus (memory/learning). Brief silence lowers stress, sharpens focus.
- Kobe on fear: "By sitting with those thoughts in silence, [I was] able to gain perspective on them. It gives you the ability to look at it for what it is, which is nothing more than your imagination running its course."
- **Author's application:** Visualize responses to daily scenarios — clingy daughter, Fortnite-obsessed son, work questions.
