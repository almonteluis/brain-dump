# Orchestration + Observability + Auditability = Governance

**Source:** raw/02_reference/approaches/orchestration-observability-auditability-governance.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-09
**Type:** Framework guide

## Summary

The three pillars of AI governance: Orchestration (strategic integration), Observability (tracing actions to outcomes), and Auditability (transparent decision trails) combine to enable responsible AI at scale.

## Key Claims

```
Orchestration + Observability + Auditability = Governance
```

- Orchestration is AI systems understanding context across domains, not just API plumbing
- Observability means tracing AI actions to inputs/outputs at each boundary
- Auditability enables decision velocity through confidence, not bottlenecks
- Companies with these capabilities move faster than competitors in manual approval chains

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Orchestration
**Not:** Plumbing — connecting APIs
**Is:** AI systems understanding context across domains with specialized orchestrator models

### 2. Observability
Can you trace AI actions to original inputs/outputs at each interface?
- If yes → Auditability → Governance

### 3. Auditability
- Every decision traceable
- Clear accountability
- Known boundaries
- Increased decision velocity

## Maturity Diagnostic

| Symptom | Gap Type |
|---------|----------|
| Different answers for AI success | Coordination |
| Metrics don't connect to outcomes | Orchestration |
| Can't explain decisions' effects | Governance/Auditability |

## Building for 2026

1. Coordination layers for specialized AI systems
2. Governance frameworks for autonomous operation
3. Measurement systems connecting AI to business outcomes
4. Foundational question: "What data can we trust?"
