# Metacognitive Reflection — Definition

**Source:** metacognitive reflection.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-05-09
**Type:** Definition note

## Summary

User-paraphrased definition of [[metacognitive-reflection]] anchored to the Hou & Tan 2025 [[insight-recall|Insight Recall]] paper.

## Definition

> Metacognitive reflection is a fancy way of saying pausing to think and reflect on what and how you're learning, then adjusting my strategies. (i.e., "thinking about one's own thinking process")

## In the Hou & Tan Paper

The paper treats metacognitive reflection as a key part of [[self-regulated-learning|self-regulated learning]] and tries to scaffold it by resurfacing past insights at the right moment ([[insight-recall|Insight Recall paradigm]]).

**Considered key to achieving deep understanding and transfer of knowledge.**
