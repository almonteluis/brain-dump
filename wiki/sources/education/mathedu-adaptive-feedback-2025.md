# Adaptive Feedback in Math Education (2025)

Research on AI-driven feedback systems that go beyond correctness checking. Traditional math feedback: "Incorrect. Try again." Adaptive feedback: "You multiplied when you should have divided — this is a rate problem, not a total problem." The difference matters because the first tells the kid they're wrong; the second tells them *why* and what to do about it.

This connects directly to [[adaptive-feedback-education]] and the feedback layer in [[project-atlas]]. The design tension: feedback that's too specific short-circuits [[productive-struggle]]. Feedback that's too vague is useless. The sweet spot is feedback that names the misconception without solving the problem — essentially [[scaffolding]] in feedback form.
