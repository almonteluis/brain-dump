# Productive Struggle via AI (Bellwether)

Paper on using AI to design and calibrate struggle-inducing tasks. The Bellwether approach: instead of relying on teacher judgment to pick the right difficulty level, use an algorithm to generate problems that hit the sweet spot between "too easy" (no learning) and "too hard" (shutdown). Automated [[productive-struggle]] design.

This is directly relevant to [[project-atlas]] — the same territory. The design challenge: [[productive-struggle]] isn't just about problem difficulty. It's about the kid's emotional state, their [[cognitive-load]] at that moment, their [[math-anxiety]] level, and whether they trust the system enough to keep trying when it gets hard. An algorithm that optimizes for difficulty alone misses all of that.
