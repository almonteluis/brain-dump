[[self-determination-theory]], and other theories help improve student motivation.

this is psychological and its fueled by 3 needs:
- **Autonomy:** Having a sense of control and choice over how they learn.
- **Competence:** Feeling capable of handling the academic material and achieving success.
- **Relatedness:** Feeling supported and connected to teachers and peers

> [!success]
> When these needs are met, the brain releases dopamine, which boosts memory, attention, and creative problem-solving. 

> [!warning]
> >  When these needs are ignored, students often experience burnout, apathy, or anxiety
