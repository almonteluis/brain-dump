# Productive Struggle from the Student Perspective

Research on productive struggle focused on the student's lived experience — not just the learning outcome, but what the kid is actually going through while they're struggling. The [[nctm]] says "support productive struggle" but doesn't specify how to tell when struggle has tipped from productive to destructive. This paper tries to operationalize that boundary.

Key for [[teach-to-one]]: the system can't read facial expressions or body language. It has to infer struggle state from behavioral signals — time on task, hint usage, response patterns. The gap between what a teacher notices ("that kid is shutting down") and what an algorithm can detect is where [[adaptive-feedback-education]] hits its ceiling.
