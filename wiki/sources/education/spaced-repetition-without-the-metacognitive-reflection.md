I was reading my notes about metacognitive reflection where we quiz without reflection, pure spaced repetition X. Do you remember the answer, not do you remember your thinking? I tie this with Parsnip when I was kind of going through the generative questions that they had for me. It was related to peppers.

When I got to the question of which pepper is the sweetest, I said yellow. I was wrong but within three to five questions later it resurfaced again. Same question. One of my choices was grayed out so I couldn't reselect that again. It would have been a great time, if I did select the option, to not show me the answer but to give me a half point. Be like, "All right you got the answer right but could you explain why this was the sweetest?" I think that will help refine. I think that would be a good use case for that.
