# Tutor CoPilot: Human-AI Collaboration in Tutoring

## Summary

Tutor CoPilot is a human-AI tutoring system that provides real-time, AI-generated suggestions to human tutors during live tutoring sessions. In a randomized controlled trial with over 900 tutors and 1,000 students, the system produced a +4 percentage-point increase in topic mastery overall, with a +9 percentage-point gain for students working with lower-rated tutors — effectively closing the quality gap between high- and low-performing tutors. The intervention costs approximately $20 per tutor per year, making it highly scalable. Tutors using the system increased their use of probing questions and reduced generic praise, indicating shifts toward more pedagogically effective interaction patterns.

## Key Claims

- Human-AI collaboration outperforms AI-only or human-only tutoring on measured learning outcomes in a real-world RCT.
- The largest benefits accrue to students of lower-rated tutors, suggesting AI can act as a **quality equalizer** in distributed tutoring networks.
- AI prompts shift tutor behavior toward deeper pedagogical strategies (probing questions, diagnostic feedback) and away from surface-level interactions (generic praise).
- Cost of ~$20/tutor/year makes large-scale deployment feasible for tutoring organizations and school districts.
- The model improves tutor behavior without replacing tutors — a critical design insight for [[Teach to One]] and similar models that rely on human facilitators.

## Why This Matters for Teach to One

Teach to One operates a model where multiple adults facilitate personalized math learning. Tutor CoPilot demonstrates that embedding AI suggestions into human tutors' workflow can significantly raise the floor on instructional quality — particularly for newer or less experienced facilitators. The $20/tutor/year cost point is relevant for district-wide rollouts, and the finding that AI shifts tutor behavior toward probing and diagnostic feedback aligns with [[metacognitive-reflection]] goals in the [[three-ring-mastery-system]].
