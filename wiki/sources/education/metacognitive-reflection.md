## Definition
Metacognitive reflection is a fancy way of saying pausing to think and reflect on what and how you’re learning, then adjusting my strategies. 

(i.e., ”thinking about one’s own thinking process”)
## In this paper 
The paper treats metacognitive reflection as a key part of self‑regulated learning and tries to scaffold it by resurfacing my past insights at the right moment (Insight Recall paradigm). 

Considered key to achieving deep understanding and transfer of knowledge.
