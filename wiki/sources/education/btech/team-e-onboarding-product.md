# Team E Onboarding

Source: [Confluence — Team E Onboarding](https://newclassrooms.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/PRODUCT/pages/1083834386/Team+E+Onboarding)

## About Team E

**Team E(xplore)** is the R&D unit within New Classrooms, residing in the larger Product Team (part of Program: Product, Academics, Insights & Analytics, Program Success), within Teach to One (also includes Tech, Sales & Marketing).

> Goal hierarchy:
> 1. Teach students math
> 2. Teach more students more math
> 3. Find ways to teach more math per student with less resources (student time)
> 4. Find ways to *find ways* to teach more math with less resources (our time)

More than half of Team E's time is focused on the **student experience** within Teach to One: Roadmaps.

## First Week / Month Goals by Role

### Frontend Engineer

- **First week**: Submit a PR for a CSS change or small component
- **First sprint/month**: Deploy a complete experiment or feature (QA → PR → Staging → Production)

### Designer

- **First week**: Create a wireframe for a high-priority experiment
- **First sprint/month**: 3+ iterations (wireframe → click-throughs → visual mockups) toward a key feature

### Researcher

- **First week**: Use dashboards and user research to develop a testable hypothesis; run desk test estimating effect-size/ROI and sample size
- **First month**: Oversee a multi-cycle experiment series with multiple iterations and data analysis
