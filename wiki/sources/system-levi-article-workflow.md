# Article Processing with Levi

**Source:** raw/06_system/LEVI-ARTICLE-WORKFLOW.md
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-09
**Type:** documentation

## Summary

Quick reference for using Levi (Claude Code) to automate article processing. Covers capture commands, processing workflows, research capabilities, and quality control checklists for AI-assisted knowledge management.

## Key Claims

- Levi can fetch, summarize, extract insights, create structured notes, and integrate articles automatically
- Commands: "Save this article", "Process this article", "Find articles about [topic]", "Process my article backlog"
- Quality control requires checking summary, key insights (3-7), related note links, personal thoughts, and article rating
- Best for initial capture and full processing; avoid for quick captures you'll process yourself or very technical articles
