# Swyx: Writing Skills Template for Devtools

**Source:** [[Thread by @swyx]]
**Author:** [[@swyx]]
**Date ingested:** 2026-04-16
**Type:** thread

## Summary

swyx highlighted Anh (@byAnhtho) as "one of the most consistent heavy hitters" in devtools HN writing, announcing that she open-sourced her writing skills template. The template captures not just writing style but editing judgment — the gap between a draft and what you actually send. Community reactions focused on the idea that structure matters more than prose, and that templates compress good taste into reusable systems.

## Key Claims

- Writing templates cut HN launch post drafting from 6 hours to 45 minutes (structure > prose)
- The "Drafted vs Sent" section is the most valuable part — it captures editing judgment, not just writing style
- Open-sourcing writing templates makes "good taste portable"
- Prompting-based approaches outperform fine-tuning for style adoption (per @matt_ambrogi's experiments)
- The template is the easy part; filling it with substance is the hard work
