## 🎯 Immediate Free Wins (Start Today)

### 1. Community Solar Bill Credits - **BIGGEST SAVINGS**

**What it is:** Sign up for free to receive bill credits from local solar farms. You get credits on your O&R bill, then pay for those credits at a discount.

**How it works:**
- Your energy delivery stays the same (no panels on your roof)
- You agree to support clean energy in the grid
- Receive monthly bill credits reducing your O&R bill
- Pay for credits at 5-20% discount
- No installation, no contracts, no fees

**Potential savings:** $20-60/month or more depending on enrollment and solar farm output

**How to enroll:**
- Visit [Solstice community solar enrollment](https://enroll.solstice.us) or search "community solar Orange & Rockland"
- Check if you're eligible (most NY customers are)
- Enroll at no cost — takes 5 minutes online
- Credits appear on your O&R bill in 1-2 billing cycles

**Why O&R supports this:** NY state requires utilities to add renewables to the grid. Community solar is the easiest way for customers to benefit from these requirements.

---

### 2. Budget Billing Program

**What it is:** Spread your payments evenly across the year to avoid seasonal spikes.

**How it works:**
- O&R uses your 12-month usage history to estimate annual cost
- Divides total by 12 for consistent monthly payment
- Prevents winter heating spikes (when bills can double or triple)
- Periodic reviews adjust payment up or down based on actual usage
- Year-end settlement: overpayments credited, underpayments applied

**Who qualifies:** Customers with at least 12 months of billing history at current address

**Potential savings:** Better budgeting, avoid $300-500 winter spikes

**How to sign up:**
- Log into [O&R My Account](https://www.oru.com/en/accounts-billing/my-account/manage-my-account?sectionId=budgetBilling)
- Call Customer Assistance: 1-877-434-4100 (weekdays 8am-7pm)
- Visit: oru.com/assistance

**Payment Agreement:** Pay off outstanding balance in manageable monthly installments

**Bill Extender:** If you're 60+ years old, get up to 45 days extension on due date

**Get Well First:** Extend due date during hospital stays

**Special Services:** Free services for residential customers needing special attention

**Apply at:** [oru.com/assistance](https://www.oru.com/en/accounts-billing/payment-assistance)

### 4. Autopay Setup

**What it is:** Automatic payment from your bank account each month.

**Benefits:**
- Never miss a payment (no late fees)
- Save time — no portals, phone calls, or mailing
- More secure than mail or phone payments
- Regulated by NY state — can't overdraw

**How to set up:**
- Log into O&R My Account
- Enroll in autopay
- Link your bank account

**Cost:** Same amount, less time

## 🏠 Energy Saving Tips (Reduce Usage)

Based on O&R's official recommendations:

### Heating & Cooling

**Set thermostat wisely:**
- Set to lowest comfortable temperature (68°F recommended for winter)
- Install programmable or smart thermostat for automatic adjustments
- Lower heat by 1°F = 3-5% savings on heating bill

**Maintain your system:**
- Keep ducts and vents clean and unobstructed
- Replace furnace/AC filters every 3 months
- Have qualified contractor clean and inspect heating systems annually
- Insulate hot-water pipes and warm-air ducts

**Solar gain:**
- Open shades/curtains on sunny winter days (free heat)
- Close them at night to retain heat
- In summer, close shades to block heat

**Savings potential:** $10-30/month depending on home size

---

### Hot Water Usage

**Shower vs bath:**
- Showers use half the water of baths
- Less hot water = less energy used

**Wash temperature:**
- Switch from hot to cold water for laundry
- Cuts energy use in half for each load
- Modern detergents work just as well in cold water

**Water heater settings:**
- Set to 120°F (saves energy and prevents scalding)
- Insulate water heater tank if it's in unheated space

**Savings potential:** $5-15/month

---

### Lighting & Electronics

**LED bulbs:**
- Replace incandescent and CFL bulbs with LEDs
- LEDs use 75% less energy and last 25x longer
- LEDs cost $2-5 each, save $5-10/year per bulb

**Phantom power:**
- Unplug electronics not in use (TVs, game consoles, chargers)
- Use smart power strips to cut power to multiple devices at once
- Phantom loads account for 5-10% of residential energy use

**Savings potential:** $5-20/month

---

### Submit Actual Meter Readings

**Problem:** O&R sometimes issues estimated bills (up to 50% of the time)

**Why estimates hurt:**
- You're charged based on guess, not actual usage
- Next actual reading can bring large catch-up bill
- Causes budget surprises

**Solution:**
- Keep your meter accessible at all times
- Submit your own meter readings online to avoid estimates
- Use O&R's [meter reading guide](https://www.oru.com/en/accounts-billing/how-to-read-your-meter)

**Benefit:** Accurate bills, no surprises

## 🌞 Long-Term Investments (Major Savings)

### Energy Efficiency Upgrades

**Insulation & Sealing:**
- Attic and wall insulation (biggest impact)
- Seal air leaks around windows, doors, outlets
- Weatherstripping on doors and windows
- **Savings:** 10-30% on heating/cooling ($20-60/month)

**Energy Star Appliances:**
- Replace old refrigerators, washers, dryers with Energy Star models
- Heat pump water heaters (2-3x more efficient than electric)
- Smart thermostats (Nest, Ecobee) — learn your schedule
- **Savings:** 10-50% per appliance replaced

**Heat Pumps:**
- Replace oil/gas furnace with cold-climate heat pump
- Federal tax credits available through Inflation Reduction Act
- **Savings:** 30-50% on heating costs

---

### NYSERDA Rebates & Programs

**NYSERDA** (New York State Energy Research and Development Authority) offers significant rebates:

**Home Energy Efficiency:**
- Free or discounted home energy assessments
- Rebates for insulation, air sealing, and equipment upgrades
- Low-income programs (EmPower+) cover 100% of costs

**Inflation Reduction Act (2025-2026):**
- Heat pump rebates (up to $8,000)
- Electric panel upgrades (up to $4,000)
- Heat pump water heaters (up to $1,750)
- Weatherization assistance

**How to apply:**
- Visit [NYSERDA Programs](https://www.nyserda.ny.gov/All-Programs)
- Take online eligibility survey
- Work with NYSERDA-approved contractors

**Savings potential:** Thousands in upfront costs + ongoing monthly savings

---

### Alternative Energy Suppliers

**NY is deregulated:** You can choose your energy supplier while O&R delivers it.

**How to save:**
- Compare electric and gas supply rates on [NY Energy Ratings](https://www.nyenergyratings.com)
- Look for fixed-rate plans lower than O&R's default
- Watch out for variable rates that spike

**Caution:**
- Read contracts carefully — early termination fees, rate guarantees
- Check total price (supply + delivery fees)
- Verify supplier is registered with NY PSC

**Potential savings:** $5-30/month depending on market rates
