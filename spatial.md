# Spatial Interview Experience

## The Technical Deep Dive with Oz: When Reality Hits

First up was Oz, the technical lead. We dove into my take-home project like it was a Christopher Nolan movie - layer by layer. There I was, trying to explain my caching function like I wrote it in my sleep (spoiler: I didn't write it at all), when Oz dropped the [[Accessibility]] bomb. Did you know Chrome DevTools has built-in [[Accessibility]] features? Honestly, that's foundational knowledge I should have already mastered. A real "you should know this by now" moment. Mind. Blown. 🤯

This connects to the [[Red Energy Foundation]] idea — foundational knowledge matters. You can't skip the basics (like accessibility in DevTools) and expect to perform at the purple/transcendent level.

## Unexpected Schedule Change

Just when I thought I had my interview prep schedule sorted (like my npm dependencies - narrator: they were not), they pulled a fast one. "Hey, can you chat with our PE... like, tomorrow?" My stomach dropped. Sure, why not? It's not like I needed those extra days of prep or anything... *sweaty palms, racing thoughts, sudden panic*

## The Process Over Outcome Lesson

This experience reinforced [[Process Over Outcome Universal Pattern]] — the interview prep process (building projects, studying fundamentals, mock interviews) is what creates readiness, not just "wanting the job." When they moved up the schedule, the process I had already done is what saved me.
