# Did Jeffrey Epstein win the Oklahoma Powerball lottery, July 2, 2008?

## Summary

Article investigates whether Jeffrey Epstein anonymously won the Oklahoma Powerball lottery through the "Zorro Trust" on July 2, 2008, collecting $29.3 million just as he began serving an 18-month jail term.

---

## Key Claims

### The Powerball Win
- **July 2, 2008:** Oklahoma Powerball winning ticket paid $29.3 million
- **Recipient:** "Zorro Trust" (anonymous trust)
- **Payout:** Received by an Oklahoma City financial planning company on behalf of Zorro Trust
- **Timing:** July 1, 2008 - Epstein started serving his 18-month jail term in Florida as Inmate No. W35755

### Zorro Trust Connection to Epstein
- **1994:** Zorro Trust of New York documented as headed by Jeffrey Epstein of NYC
- Epstein purchased 6,270+ acres and water rights in New Mexico through Zorro Trust
- **Zorro Ranch:** Planned and managed by Epstein
- **Trust Manager:** Brice M. Gordon (also Ranch Manager)
- **Contact:** zorro.office@gmail.com
- Gordon appears in Epstein's "New Mexico" black book listings

### Pilot's Deposition
Larry Visoski (Epstein's pilot) deposition referenced:
> "The Zorro Trust winning an 85 million dollar — claiming the ticket for 85 million dollar Powerball ticket in 2008."

*(Note: Article mentions both $29.3M and $85M figures)*

## Additional Epstein Connections Covered

### Office of Foreign Missions (US State Department)
- **1992:** OFM leased a "small castle" at 34 East 69th Street, Manhattan to Epstein
- **Rent:** $15,000/month
- **Lease Period:** Originally 2 years (1992-1994), extended to 1997
- **Eviction:** July 16, 1998 - federal lawsuit, Epstein sued in Nov 1996
- **Point of Contact:** Richard C. Massey, OFM official
- **Purpose Unclear:** OFM mandate is serving foreign diplomatic community - Epstein was a US citizen

### Bill Clinton Connection
- **January 30, 1992:** Epstein donated $1,000 to Bill Clinton
- **February 1, 1992:** Epstein moved into OFM-leased property (next day)
- **2003:** Epstein, Harvey Weinstein, and Mortimer Zuckerman bought New York Magazine

### Ponzi Scheme Connections
- **Steven Hoffenberg:** Epstein's mentor, involved in $460M Ponzi scheme (1995 - largest ever at the time)
- Hoffenberg claimed Epstein was his co-conspirator and Epstein's fortune was built on Towers Financial fraud
- Epstein listed as interested party in $1.4B Rothstein Rosenfeldt Adler Ponzi scheme
- Hoffenberg quote: "He was great at moving money illegally... He was the deeper architect"
