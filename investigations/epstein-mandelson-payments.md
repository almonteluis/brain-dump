# Jeffrey Epstein sent $75,000 to Lord Mandelson linked accounts, documents appear to show

## Summary

Jeffrey Epstein made three payments totaling $75,000 (£55,000) to accounts connected to Lord Mandelson (Peter Mandelson) between 2003 and 2004, according to bank statements released by the US Department of Justice.

---

## Key Facts

### The Payments
- **Period:** 2003-2004
- **Total:** $75,000 (£55,000)
- **Structure:** Three separate $25,000 payments

### Payment Details
1. **May 14, 2003:** $25,000 sent to Barclays bank account
   - Account holder: Reinaldo Avila da Silva (Lord Mandelson's partner at the time)
   - Beneficiary: "Peter Mandelson"

2. **June 2004 (two payments):** $25,000 each to HSBC accounts
   - Beneficiary: "Peter Mandelson" named as BEN (beneficiary)

**Note:** It's unclear if payments actually made it into the accounts.

---

## Lord Mandelson's Response

### Statements
- **No recollection:** Has "no record or recollection of receiving sums"
- **Authenticity:** Cannot verify if documents are authentic
- **Apology:** Regretted "ever having known Epstein" and continuing association after his 2008 conviction
- **Apology to victims:** Apologized "unequivocally to women and girls who suffered"

### Ambassadors Role
- Appointed UK ambassador to US by Sir Keir Starmer in December 2024
- **Sacked September 2025:** Following further revelations about Epstein friendship
- **Reason for removal:** "Things he had not disclosed" to government
- **Government knowledge:** Housing Secretary Steve Reed confirmed government was not aware of alleged financial links
