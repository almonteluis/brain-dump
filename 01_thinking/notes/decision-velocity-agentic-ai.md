# Decision Velocity in Agentic AI

## Definition

**Decision Velocity** = How quickly smaller decision trees and processes can be automated at scale

It's a useful lens for understanding what changes when [[Orchestration Observability Auditability Governance]] work together.

## Traditional Decision Loop

1. Someone identifies an issue
2. Gathers data
3. Analyzes options
4. Escalates to whoever has authority
5. That person reviews context
6. Makes a call
7. Communicates decision
8. Implementation happens
9. Results monitored

**Each step involves coordination costs:** finding the right person, explaining context, waiting for availability, following up on execution.

## AI-Augmented Decision Loop

AI and Agents handle the entire loop — including execution and monitoring.

**But this only works if:**
- Agent understands boundaries it operates within (governance)
- Agent can coordinate with other systems that need to know about the decision (orchestration)

## The Impact

Companies getting this right achieve **5-7x improvements** in decision cycles.

Not 10% better — **multiple times faster**.

- Respond to market changes: weeks → days
- Adjust operations: quarterly → nearly continuously

## Why It Requires Governance

High decision velocity without governance = chaos

High decision velocity WITH governance = competitive advantage

The governance/observability layer is what gives you confidence to ship faster. You're not slowing down — you're removing the friction of uncertainty.

## The Equation

```
Decision Velocity = (Automation + Coordination) × Confidence
```

Where confidence comes from observability and auditability.
