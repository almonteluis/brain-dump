# AI as Management Skill

## The Insight

> "AI isn't a tool skill, it's a management skill."

The people who integrate well with AI aren't just good at prompting or coding — they're good at specific "senior-level" skills.

## The 6 Skills That Matter

**Source**: Microsoft study via @Hesamation

### 1. Context Assembly

Knowing **what information to provide** from **which sources** and **why**.

AI is sensitive about context quality. Garbage in, garbage out applies to thinking, not just data.

### 2. Quality Judgement

You must know **when to trust AI output** and **how to verify it**.

Which parts are likely reliable? Which are hallucinated?

### 3. Task Decomposition

**Avoid throwing entire projects** at AI.

Break into manageable chunks. Delegate to right number of agents with right skills. Orchestrate. Quantify output.

### 4. Iterative Refinement

Do you:
- Trust that first output?
- Abandon the whole task?
- Treat it as a starting point?

The mindset shift from "prompting good" to "managing AI" happened gradually. People good at AI are learning to become good managers/seniors without explicitly realizing.

### 5. Workflow Integration

Do you treat it as:
- A side tool?
- An integrated capability?

The difference determines effectiveness.

### 6. Frontier Recognition

Knowing when you're **operating outside AI capabilities**.

## The Management Mindset

> "Just as you wouldn't take a whole app idea and give it to an intern and expect anything to work, you need to chunk up the problem into small pieces, delegate them to the right number of agents with the right skills, orchestrate them, and quantify their output."

**The shift**: From "how do I prompt better?" to "how do I manage AI like a team?"

## Tinkerers vs Execs

**By**: @nikunj

**Advice to CEOs**:
- Hire tinkerers
- Make it high status
- Find ones who explore edges and are gifted at teaching
- Empower them to roam org and fix automatable problems

**What execs will say**:
- "They don't understand scale"
- "What about rollout?"
- "This isn't systemic"

**Your response**: Listen, and ignore. Or give them budget to hire their own tinkerer to achieve their targets.

**Give tinkerers**: Ambition, purpose, hard targets

**Watch them**: Fly.
