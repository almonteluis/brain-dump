# AI Coordination Gap

## The Problem

A client had 17 different AI implementations:
- Marketing automation
- Supply chain optimization
- HR screening
- Customer service chatbot
- Product recommendation engine
- Engineering code reviews
- Testing automation

**Each worked fine in isolation.** But when the product team tried to launch Agents that operations and the business couldn't observe and audit, they revealed existential risks and blindsides.

Nobody had designed these systems to work together because nobody thought about orchestration until it was too late.

## The Three Gaps

| Gap Type | Symptom | Question to Ask |
|----------|---------|-----------------|
| **Coordination** | Everyone gives different answers for AI success | What does success look like? |
| **Orchestration** | Metrics don't connect to business outcomes | How does this connect to business value? |
| **Governance/Auditability** | Can't explain AI decisions' effects on other systems | How do AI decisions affect other systems? |

## Root Cause

Most AI initiatives start with:
- Individual teams building individual solutions
- Local optimization (what helps MY team?)
- No coordination layer
- No shared data foundation
- No common governance framework

This works for experimentation but doesn't scale.

## The Fix

Orchestration means strategic integration, not just APIs:
- Systems understand context across domains
- Coordination layer manages specialized components
- Objectives are aligned across teams
- Governance is built in, not bolted on

## The Competitive Reality

MIT research shows:
- **Early AI maturity**: Financial performance below industry average
- **Advanced AI maturity**: Financial performance well above average

The difference isn't having more AI — it's having the capabilities to use it strategically.
